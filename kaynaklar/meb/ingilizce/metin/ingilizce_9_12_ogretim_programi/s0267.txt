DIFFERENTIATION
Expansion
Objective: To talk about learning activities in the classroom and learning with technology in the classroom in 
English through a drama technique by using target vocabulary.
Activity 1: Classroom Drama Box
The teacher prepares classroom situations, such as forgetting an assignment, asking for help, or explaining 
how to use a laptop, and gives each group a different scenario. Students work in pairs or small groups to 
create a short dialogue using target vocabulary (e.g., assignments, brainstorming, analyses) and grammar 
(e.g., modal verbs, reflexive pronouns). They act out their dialogue in front of the class, using expressions 
like ''Can I explain something?'' or ''I’ll summarise the main points''. After each role-play, the teacher and 
classmates provide feedback on pronunciation, vocabulary use, fluency, and creativity. Students reflect on 
their thoughts and feelings about the task.
Supporting
Objective: To talk about learning activities in the classroom and learning with technology in the classroom in 
English through a matching and fill-in-the-blanks activity by using target vocabulary.
Activity 1: Match & Complete
The teacher provides a worksheet to the students. In the first section, students match pictures with the 
corresponding vocabulary words. For example, a picture of a laptop is matched with the word ''laptop''. In 
the second section, after understanding the meanings of the words, students complete a fill-in-the-blanks 
activity using a word bank (assignments, pair work, role-play, laptop, password, brainstorming, interactive 
activity, printer). Example sentences may include: ''Our teacher gave us three ___ to complete by next week'', 
or ''Can I use the ___ to print my report?''. At the end of the activity, the teacher checks the answers with the 
class, explains the correct options, and provides constructive feedback. Students reflect on their thoughts 
and feelings about the task.
