THE ENGLISH LANGUAGE CURRICULUM
CONTENT FRAME  
PERSONAL LIFE AND WELL-BEING  
Sub-themes: ''Providing personal well-being through social health; providing personal well-being with 
economic stability (fashion addicts, shopaholics)''
Target Vocabulary in Use (with revisional vocabulary): 
Vocabulary for providing personal well-being through social health: 
Nouns: Emotional intelligence, a belief, a difficulty, an attitude, an occasion, a friendship, a value.
Verbs: To communicate effectively, to admire, to admit, to advise, to fulfil needs, to strengthen bonds, to 
handle disagreements.
Phrasal verbs: To get over.
Adjectives: Engaged, practical, gentle, qualified, involved.
Adverbs: Naturally, fairly.
Vocabulary for providing personal well-being with economic stability (Fashion addicts, 
shopaholics): 
Nouns: Latest trends, new collections, a shopaholic, fashion, a shopping centre (mall-AmE), a version, 
marketing, luxury.
Verbs: To stick to the list, to limit social media, to invest, to wonder, to treat, to supply, to pretend, to get 
attraction.
Phrasal Verbs: To keep up with.
Adjectives: Old-fashioned, unnecessary, unpleasant, coloured.
Adverbs: Necessarily, perfectly.

*Local Cultural, Social and  Moral Considerations of the Theme
In this theme, specific examples, cases, situations, events, and practices reflecting Turkish social, cultural, 
and moral customs, traditions, and heritage  will be included across all sub-topics. This consideration will be 
age-and-level appropriate and selected with the Turkish learners’ social and cultural context in mind.  A table 
of contents for this section is provided in the introduction to the whole English Language Curriculum.
Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-The Simple Past Tense: (They limited their social media usage.)
-The Past Progressive Tense: (She was jogging at seven o’clock yesterday.)
-The Past Perfect Tense: (They attended a wellness program after they had gone bankrupt.)
-The Past Perfect Progressive Tense: (She had been investing money for her future before she was in debt.)
Functions of the Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Use of the Simple Past Tense: Describing an action that was completed at a specific moment in the past for 
revision.
-Use of the Past Progressive Tense: Describing an action that was happening at a specific moment in the past.
-Use of the Past Perfect Tense: Describing an action that was completed before another action in the past 
for revision.
-Use of the Past Perfect Progressive Tense: Describing an action that was happening before another action 
in the past for revision.
