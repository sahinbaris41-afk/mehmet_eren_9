Generalisations/Key Concepts 
Generalisations
The concept of ''classroom life where classmates build up friendships and exchange information about their 
daily and study routines, habits and activities in class and at home'' is present in all languages because it is 
one of the well-known facts that almost all people, with some exceptions, go to school and meet different 
people from different daily and study routines, habits, and activities. Majority of children and young people 
attend school classes where they build up friendships with their classmates by exchanging information about 
themselves in terms of their daily and study routines, habits and activities in class and at home. Engaging in 
such conversations allows them to learn about each other and build up stronger relationships.
Key Concepts
Classroom life; daily routines, habits, and activities; study routines, habits, and activities; students with 
different daily routines/habits/activities, students with different study routines/habits/activities.
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation)
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Preparing self- recordings (visual, audio) at school/at home. Presenting 
a recording that displays her/his study and daily routines, habits and activities in class and at home or 
conducting an interview with a classmate and talking about her/ his study and daily routines, habits and 
activities in class and at home. The assignment is evaluated using rubrics.
LEARNING-TEACHING EXPERIENCES
Basic Acceptations: Preparedness/Readiness
It is generally accepted that people who start to learn a new language bring with them their pre-existing 
knowledge and transfer it to their new language learning. For instance, this theme is based on the concept of 
''classroom life where classmates build up friendships and exchange information about their daily and study 
routines, habits and activities in class and at home. As students in year 9 (nine) of upper-secondary school, 
they are expected to know what a classroom life where they meet different classmates is, what a daily 
routine/habit/ activity is, what a study routine/habit/activity is in their own language. They are also aware 
that there are several students from different backgrounds with different daily routines/habits/activities 
and study routines/habits/activities. Students can think about and express such situations in their own 
languages, but they may not know how to say them in a foreign language if they have not been covered at all
