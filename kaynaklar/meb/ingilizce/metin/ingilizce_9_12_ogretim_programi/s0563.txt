*Local Cultural, Social and  Moral Considerations of the Theme
In this theme, specific examples, cases, situations, events, and practices reflecting Turkish social, cultural, 
and moral customs, traditions, and heritage  will be included across all sub-topics. This consideration will be 
age-and-level appropriate and selected with the Turkish learners’ social and cultural context in mind.  A table 
of contents for this section is provided in the introduction to the whole English Language Curriculum.
Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-The Future Progressive Tense: (Companies will be trading across borders more frequently. People will be 
adapting to new cultural diversity in a globalised world.)
-Passive Voice of the Future-Progressive Tense: (Cross-cultural events will be being organised to promote 
cultural exchange. Excessive energy will be being consumed due to the rise in global temperatures.)
Functions of the Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Use of the Future Progressive Tense: Describing ongoing actions in the future.
-Use of Passive Voice of the Future -Progressive Tense: Describing ongoing passive actions in the future.

Generalisations/Key Concepts   
Generalisations
The target concept of this theme ''globalisation, problems and solutions; environmental issues and solutions'' 
is present in almost all languages. Students who start to learn a new language inevitably bring with them 
their pre-existing knowledge and transfer it to their new language learning context. For example, a student 
in an upper secondary school may know what ''globalisation, problems and solutions; environmental issues 
and solutions'' means, but may not know how to say these things in another language if this has not yet been 
covered in their existing curriculum. However, this existing knowledge in their own language will help them 
recognise, understand, and learn the new information more easily in a foreign language, in this case English.
Key Concepts
Globalisation, global problems, solutions; environmental issues.
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation)

global problems such as urbanisation, climate change, and inequality, their impacts, and possible solutions.

environmental challenges such as pollution and melting ice caps, and suggest solutions such as recycling, 
zero-waste practices, and policies for sustainability. Each student will write a short report explaining one
