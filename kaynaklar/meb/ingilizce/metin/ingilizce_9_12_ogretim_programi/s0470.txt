Generalisations/Key Concepts  
Generalisations
The target concept of this theme ''personal life and well-being with its components such as providing well-
being with physical health (body and illnesses); providing well-being with mental and emotional health'' is 
present in almost all languages. Students who start to learn a new language inevitably bring with them their 
pre-existing knowledge and transfer it to their new language learning context. For example, a student in an 
upper secondary school may know what ''personal life and well-being with its components such as providing 
well-being with physical health (body and illnesses); providing well-being with mental and emotional health'' 
mean, but may not know how to say these things in another language if this has not yet been covered in 
their existing curriculum. However, this existing knowledge in their own language will help them recognise, 
understand, and learn the new information more easily in a foreign language, in this case English.
Key Concepts
Personal life,  well-being, well-being with physical health, body and illnesses, providing well-being with mental 
and emotional health.
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation) 
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Students will explore the theme of ''Personal Life and Well-Being'' by focusing 
on how both physical and mental/emotional health contribute to overall well-being. Students are asked to 
research and present a comprehensive project that addresses two key aspects: maintaining physical health 
and nurturing mental and emotional health. They will analyse the importance of balancing both aspects 
to ensure a healthy lifestyle and discuss the ways in which physical and mental health are interconnected. 
Students will create a multimedia presentation (such as a video, slideshow) that highlights personal strategies 
for improving well-being, reflects on how their own habits affect their health, and includes advice or tips on 
maintaining physical and emotional balance. The final presentation should also include a reflection on how 
social pressures, technology, and modern life impact well-being, offering a critical look at how to navigate 
these challenges for better health. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, 
and/or a marking scheme.
