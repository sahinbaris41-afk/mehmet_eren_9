Generalisations/Key Concepts  
Generalisations
The target concept of this theme ''classroom life and learning with its components such as comparing 
different learning activities and styles; comparing traditional education and modern education with 
technology'' is present in almost all languages. Students who start to learn a new language inevitably bring 
with them their pre-existing knowledge and transfer it to their new language learning context. For example, a 
student in an upper secondary school may know what ''classroom life and learning with its components such 
as comparing different learning activities and styles; comparing traditional education and modern education 
with technology'' means but may not know how to say these things in another language if this has not yet been 
covered in their existing curriculum. However, this existing knowledge in their own language will help them 
recognise, understand, and learn the new information more easily in a foreign language, in this case English.
Key Concepts
Classroom life, learning, comparing different learning activities and styles, traditional education, modern 
education, comparing traditional education and modern education with technology, technology.
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation) 
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Students will create a project titled ''Classroom Life and Learning: Tradition 
Meets Innovation'' exploring different aspects of learning activities, styles, and educational approaches. First, 
students will compare various learning activities and personal learning styles, such as auditory, visual, and 
kinesthetic learning, reflecting on how these styles affect individual and group learning experiences. They 
will then examine the differences between traditional education methods and modern education enhanced 
by technology, analysing the benefits and challenges of each approach. Students should include real-life 
examples, such as traditional lectures versus interactive smart board lessons, or handwritten notes versus 
digital tools like tablets and apps. The project will consist of a written report and a creative presentation (e.g., 
a slideshow, video, or poster) that includes comparisons, personal reflections, and visual aids. Students will 
present their findings in class, showcasing how learning has evolved and how technology can enhance or 
complement traditional education. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, 
and/or a marking scheme.
