Generalisations/Key Concepts
Generalisations
The target concept of this theme ''the impact of technology on family life; the relationships between the 
family and community'' is present in almost all languages. Students who start to learn a new language 
inevitably bring with them their pre-existing knowledge and transfer it to their new language learning 
context. For example, a student in an upper secondary school may know what ''the impact of technology on 
family life; the relationships between the family and community'' means, but may not know how to say these 
things in a foreign language if this has not yet been covered in their existing curriculum. However, this existing 
knowledge in their own language will help them recognise, understand, and learn the new information more 
easily in a foreign language, in this case English.
Key Concepts
Family life, the impact of technology, the impact of technology on family life, community, the relationships 
between the family and community. 
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation) 
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Students will create a digital story exploring the theme ''Family Life and 
Home'' with a focus on the impact of technology on family life and the relationships between the family and 
community. They will illustrate how modern technologies, such as smartphones, social media, and smart 
home devices, have changed the way families interact, both positively and negatively. Students will also 
examine how families connect with their communities, considering the role of technology in enhancing or 
hindering these relationships. The project should creatively describe real-life or scenarios that highlight 
the balance between embracing technology and maintaining meaningful connections within the family and 
community. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a marking 
scheme.
