Objective: To help students identify and categorise geographical features, environmental issues, and 
solutions in English by using target vocabulary.
Activity 1: Categorize and Match: Environmental Awareness
The teacher shows flashcards featuring geographical features such as ''beach, forest, and glacier'' and 
asks students to name them aloud, repeating the words for clarity. Next, s/he gives a worksheet with 
vocabulary items such as ''ozone layer, climate change, and to recycle'' where students categorise the 
words under ''Geographical Features'', ''Environmental Issues'', and ''Solutions''. For example, they match 
''forest'' to ''Geographical Features'', ''climate change'' to ''Environmental Issues'', and ''to recycle'' to ''Solutions''. 
After completing the task, students read their matches aloud. The teacher listens, provides feedback, and 
corrects any misplacements. Students reflect on their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
