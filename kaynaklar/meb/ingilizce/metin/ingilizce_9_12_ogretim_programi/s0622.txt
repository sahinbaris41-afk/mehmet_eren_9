THE ENGLISH LANGUAGE CURRICULUM
Activity 1: Role-Playing Solutions
The teacher tells the students that they role-play. S/He introduces the concept of addressing school-related 
challenges. The importance of understanding challenges such as exam stress, burnout, or lack of confidence 
is highlighted, along with effective strategies such as time management, goal setting, and self-discipline. 
Students work in pairs; one role represents a student experiencing challenges, while the other represents 
a teacher or counsellor offering guidance. The dialogue includes examples of challenges and strategies, 
incorporating key grammar points such as adverbial phrases (e.g., ''Tasks are completed efficiently in the 
morning'') and passive voice (e.g., ''Support is often provided by peers and mentors''). After preparation, pairs 
present their role-plays to the group. The teacher listens carefully and provides constructive feedback on 
target vocabulary usage, grammar accuracy, and pronunciation. Students reflect on their thoughts and 
feelings about the activity.
Supporting
Objective: To help students identify and categorise challenges and coping strategies in English by using 
target vocabulary. 
Activity 1: Overcoming Challenges
The teacher prepares a reading text for this activity. Students read the following text or another text like that 
and underline the challenges and coping strategies mentioned:
''Students sometimes face some challenges when studying. They learn how to set goals and manage their 
time. Some students take breaks to avoid stress. Teachers help them stay focused and complete tasks by 
giving advice and support. Self-discipline is also important to overcome problems''.
After underlining, students categorise the words they identified:
Challenges: Tiredness, stress
Coping Strategies: set goals, manage time, take breaks, stay focused, self-discipline
The teacher checks the categories and provides constructive and supportive feedback on pronunciation and 
accuracy. Students reflect on their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
