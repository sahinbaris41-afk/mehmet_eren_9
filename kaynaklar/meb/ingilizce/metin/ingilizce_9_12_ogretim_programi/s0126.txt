Supporting
Objective: To describe family members’ jobs and workplaces in English by using target vocabulary and target 
grammar.
Activity 1: Job and Workplace Pictionary
The teacher writes job titles and workplace-related vocabulary (e.g., doctor, office, mechanic, factory) on 
small pieces of paper, folds them, and places them in a box. The class is divided into teams. One student 
from each team picks a piece of paper from the box and tries to explain the job or workplace by drawing it 
on the board, without speaking. The teammates must guess the job or workplace being drawn. Once they 
guess correctly, the team must use the job or workplace in a sentence describing a family member (e.g., ''My 
uncle is a doctor. He works at a hospital''). Points are awarded for each correct guess and a properly formed 
sentence. The team with the highest score at the end wins. The teacher assesses based on fluency, the 
number of correct guesses and sentences formed with accurate grammar and vocabulary. Students reflect 
on their thoughts and feelings about the activity.

grammar.
Activity 2: Listening Practice - Daily Routines
The teacher plays short audio clips that describe different people’s daily work routines, including details about 
their jobs and workplaces. Students listen carefully and complete a worksheet, where they must identify the 
job and workplace mentioned in each clip (e.g., ''He works as a teacher at a high school. He starts his day at 
8 am.''). After listening, the class reviews the answers together. The teacher evaluates the students based 
on their skills to recognise key vocabulary and correctly identify the job and workplace in each listening task. 
Students reflect on their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
