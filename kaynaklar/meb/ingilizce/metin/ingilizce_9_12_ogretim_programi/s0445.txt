*About “Background Grammatical Structures in Use”:
The “Background Grammatical Structures” in this section are not only revisional but also serve as contextual 
support and contribute to meaning-making. They are intended to be used within the theme to help students 
grasp the meaning of the language constructed through the use of “Target Grammatical Structures” without 
being explicitly taught. These structures are not considered "Target Grammatical Structures” themselves 
rather they function as a part of the facilitation process, supporting both the target forms and the contexts in 
which they are used. They provide opportunities for learning through revised and scaffolded language.
Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Simple Present Tense for Future: (The interactive competition starts at 9 tomorrow morning.)
-Present Progressive for Future: (I am installing antivirus software tomorrow.)
-Passive Voice of Future Tenses: (The smart board will be updated next week.)
-Will for asking for help: (Will you do this task for me?)
-Could for polite requests: (Could you fill out this form, please?)
Functions of the Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Use of ‘‘will’’ for sudden and spontaneous decisions: Describing sudden and spontaneous decisions.
-Use of the Future Simple vs. Be going to Future: Describing spontaneous decisions, promises, or predictions 
based on opinions, and planned actions or predictions based on evidence.
-Use of the Simple Present Tense for Future: Describing future events that are part of a fixed schedule or 
timetable.
-Use of the Present Progressive for Future: Describing actions that are already planned or decided to take 
place at a future time.
