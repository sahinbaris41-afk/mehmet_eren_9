9. Evaluating participation and engagement in group work: Objective: To collaborate and participate 
group work activities meaningfully and efficiently by using relevant and appropriate target 
vocabulary, pronunciation, and grammar in context. Indicators: Effective performance of roles, 
participation, contribution to group outcomes, efficient and effectively and meaningfully. Frequency: 
Monthly during and after observation and monitoring sessions. Evaluation/Assessment tools: An 
observation checklist, a rating scale, and/or rubrics. Evaluation/Assessment focus: Evaluation is 
based on collaboration, content relevance, accurate and meaningful use of language with target 
vocabulary, pronunciation, and grammar in context, and successful engagement in communication 
process by encoding and decoding the messages accurately and meaningfully.
10. Evaluating participation and engagement in drama work: Objectives: To present, engage in and 
participate in drama activities such as pantomime stories, role plays, plays and presentations by 
using effective and meaningful language with target vocabulary and/or the target grammar through 
acting. Indicators: Engagement, creativity, rapport with the audience, accurate and meaningful use 
of language with target vocabulary, pronunciation, and grammar in context. Frequency: Monthly 
during and after observation and monitoring sessions. Evaluation/Assessment tools: Rubrics, a 
rating scale, and/ or a checklist. Evaluation/Assessment focus: Evaluation is based on collaboration, 
content relevance, accurate and meaningful use of language with target vocabulary, pronunciation, 
and grammar in context, presentation skills, successful engagement in communication process by 
encoding and decoding the messages accurately and meaningfully.
11. Evaluating self-productions: Objective: To prepare and produce personal products such as digital 
diaries/journals, voice recordings, visual recording, projects, portfolios, short films, documentaries, 
scenarios, blogs, vlogs and so on related to the target theme including the use of target vocabulary, 
pronunciation, and grammar at school and at home, and to present them to the class at school. 
Indicators: Content relevance, meaningful and accurate use of language with target vocabulary, 
pronunciation, and grammar in context, accurate, efficient and effective use of supplementary 
materials. Frequency: Monthly during and after observation and monitoring sessions. Evaluation/ 
Assessment tools: Rubrics, a rating scale, a written feedback report and/ or a checklist. Evaluation/
Assessment focus: Evaluation is based on material preparation skills, creativity, organisation, 
content relevance, coherence, presentation skills, effort, originality, accurate and meaningful use 
of language with target vocabulary, pronunciation, and grammar, and effective communication 
process.
12. Evaluating writing skills: Objective: To produce written tasks by developing portfolios including 
sentences, short texts, notes, paragraphs, essays, letters, reviews, response papers, fact files, emails, 
messages, plans, programmes, timetables, projects, reflective diaries/journals, posters, etc., by using 
target vocabulary and grammar meaningfully and accurately. Indicators: Engagement, creativity, 
coherence, content relevancy, accurate and meaningful use of language with target vocabulary, 
pronunciation, and grammar in context, effective presentation skills. Frequency: Every two weeks. 
Evaluation/Assessment tools: Rubrics or a marking scheme. Evaluation/Assessment focus: 
Evaluation will be based on organisation, creativity, originality, effort, content relevance, accurate and 
meaningful use of language with target vocabulary and grammar, and presentation skills.
