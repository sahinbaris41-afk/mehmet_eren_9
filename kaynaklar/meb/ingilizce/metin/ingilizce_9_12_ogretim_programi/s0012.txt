scope, the objectives concerning students are as follows::
1) They are expected to:
a) recognise, perceive, identify, understand, and interpret oral/verbal (auditory) and written (visual) 
content, which contains the language with target language structures, vocabulary and social 
language correctly and efficiently.
b) apply this knowledge and related skills to their own lives and use it effectively in oral/verbal or 
written communication in English
c) use this knowledge and related skills to understand social and cultural differences to 
communicate with other people effectively in English.
2) They are expected to find remedies for the problems and issues in the content by recognising, 
perceiving, and making sense of the functions of the language with the target language structures, 
words and social language that they are learning, and to express these remedies.
3) In case of a language communication problem with others, they are expected to share the solution 
with a content that includes the language with the target language structures, words and social 
language functioning well and can contribute to healthy communication.
4) They are expected to understand and respect the content including rules, principles, situations, 
thoughts, feelings, and values of other cultures.
5) In situations that require provision of information and/or discussion of content, they are expected 
to be able to convey this information in a language that includes target language structures, words 
and social language in a form that they function accurately, and others perceive and understand it.
6) When discussing ideas and opinions, they are expected to justify their ideas and opinions in a 
consistent and valid way in communication with others by using understandable, accurate and 
appropriate English.
7) They are expected to use the content in English automatically, authentically, naturally and 
appropriately in the necessary and appropriate contexts at school and in their personal and social 
lives outside school.
8) They are expected to have a holistic perspective in understanding different information, emotions, 
thoughts and justifications with the help of language skills, the accumulation of knowledge, thoughts, 
and opinions they have gained as a result of understanding, perceiving, analysing, synthesising and 
evaluating the content, and to reflect this perspective in communicative exchanges and discussions 
they have with others in English.
9) They are expected to acquire social-emotional skills, literacy skills, higher-order thinking skills and 
values by integrating them with English language learning skills.
10) They are expected to use all the language skills and other skills integrated with English language 
skills that they have acquired to improve and develop their own and others’ living conditions; to use 
them for the benefit and development of their country; to use them to develop living conditions in 
the world and in nature; to play an active role in designing and shaping the future by using these skills 
to develop innovative and creative ideas.
