DIFFERENTIATION
Expansion
Objective: To talk about types of cities, social life in the rural areas and cities in English by using target 
vocabulary and target grammar.
Activity 1: Match, Identify, and Build up
The teacher introduces the activity. Students start by matching a picture to its descriptive sentence. For 
example, you might see a picture of a green park and match it with the description ''a relaxing and refreshing 
area with trees and fresh air''. Once they’ve made the match, identify the related word, like ''green city''. Then, 
write their own sentence using the word, such as, ''This green city has many parks for people to enjoy''. The 
teacher provides feedback on accuracy, use of target vocabulary, and pronunciation. Students reflect on 
their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
Supporting
Objective: To describe types of cities, social life in the rural areas and cities in English by using target 
vocabulary.
Activity 1: Picture Match and Speak
The teacher introduces the activity. S/he shows a series of pictures related to types of cities, social life in 
the rural areas and cities, and the vocabulary words and explains the task. ''Match each vocabulary word with 
the correct picture and then create a sentence about the picture''. For example, if you see a park with many 
trees, you could say, ''The green city has many parks, and people can relax there''. Then, the teacher gives 
constructive and supportive feedback on accuracy and pronunciation. Students reflect on their thoughts 
and feelings about the activity.
