problems, and solutions that are interesting, related to everyday life. These activities should also be

when using English in communication with others. Performance and proficiency in the use of the 
language should be assessed/evaluated according to these criteria. Learners should be provided with 
feedback on the linguistic, cultural, and social formation of their performance which is non-judgemental, 
motivational, supportive, and facilitative of their learning. Throughout the whole assessment/evaluation 
process, digital technologies can be used to make the applications and implementation more practical 
and functional.
• Alternative assessment and evaluation techniques and activities for the upper secondary classes 
are given below. There are different assessment and evaluation applications for each language skill. 
Teachers do not have to use all of the items in every lesson. They can be selected and used according to 
the needs and flow of the lesson.
1. Evaluating listening/watching and comprehension: Objective: To listen/watch efficiently for 
comprehension to be able to engage in spoken language from people or technology-based digital 
audio-visual materials to develop critical thinking skills by using various strategies and techniques 
such as listening for gist, listening for scanning, dictation, listening and note taking, collaborative 
discussions and so on. Indicators: Accurate understanding of the spoken content, responses to 
closed, semi-closed or open-ended questions in comprehension activities, successful participation 
and turn taking in various listening activities. Frequency: At least once in a month, on a regular 
basis. Evaluation tools: An observation checklist and/or observation grids for monthly monitoring 
sessions. Evaluation/Assessment focus: Evaluation is based on effective comprehension, 
inference skills, understanding of the main idea, attention to details, interpretation skills and 
successful engagement in communication by encoding and decoding the messages accurately and 
meaningfully.
2. Evaluating the use of pronunciation: Objective: To pronounce the target sounds, words, and 
phrases accurately and appropriately in context. Indicators: Clarity in articulation of target 
sounds, and phrases, appropriate and correct intonation and stress patterns and improvement in 
pronunciation over time. Frequency: Regularly, once in a month. Evaluation tools: An observation 
checklist and/or observation grids for monthly monitoring sessions. Evaluation/Assessment focus: 
Evaluation is based on accurate pronunciation of words, phrases and sentences, accurate use of 
intonation and stress patterns when speaking to/with others in the communication process, by 
using a checklist with descriptors such as ‘’clear”, ‘’moderate”, ‘’needs to improve” and by giving oral 
feedback or through written notes for observation and monitoring sessions.
3. Evaluating the use of the target vocabulary: Objective: To select and use the target vocabulary 
accurately, meaningfully, and communicatively in context. Indicators: Accurate word choice, 
accurate and appropriate use of word types such as antonyms, synonyms, and word forms, 
integration of vocabulary in speaking and writing tasks accurately and appropriately in context. 
Frequency: During or after observation and monitoring on a regular basis, every two months. 
Evaluation/Assessment tools: A checklist, a rating scale, and/or rubrics. Evaluation/Assessment 
focus: Evaluation is based on the performance in using the target vocabulary in collaborative 
and communicative activities such as role play, dialogues, discussions, debates, presentations or 
individual activities such as writing paragraphs, stories, poems, summaries, fact files and taking 
part in vocabulary jigsaws, etc.
