DIFFERENTIATION
Expansion
Objective: To talk about human nature and human well-being today and in the future; fashion and personal 
fashion preferences in English by using target vocabulary and target grammar.
Activity 1: Future Well-being Plan
Students individually create a future lifestyle plan that focuses on personal health, fashion preferences, 
and sustainability, including details about diet, exercise, digital habits, and fashion choices. For example, 
a student might write, ''In 2030, I will exercise five times a week to stay healthy and choose sustainable, 
cotton-based clothing to protect the environment''. After preparing their plans, students present them in 
small groups, comparing their ideas using sentences such as, ''This plan is more suitable (sustainable) than 
my current lifestyle'', or ''It will be as trendy as fast fashion but more eco-friendly''. In the end, the teacher 
gives feedback on pronunciation, the use of target vocabulary, and target grammar. The students reflect on 
their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
Supporting
Objective: To talk about human illnesses, diseases and give advice in English through a role play activity by 
using the target vocabulary.
Activity 1: Doctor & Patient Role-Play
Students work in pairs with predetermined roles: Student A as the ''Doctor'' and Student B as the ''Patient''. 
The teacher prepares papers with symptoms and related advice (e.g., ''headache—take a painkiller'') and 
places them in a box. Each pair selects a paper. The ''Patient'' uses the symptom to explain their problem (e.g., 
''I have a headache''), and the ''Doctor'' gives advice based on the paper (e.g., ''You should take a painkiller''). 
After each pair performs, the teacher and other students provide constructive and supportive feedback on 
pronunciation and sentence accuracy. The students reflect on their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
