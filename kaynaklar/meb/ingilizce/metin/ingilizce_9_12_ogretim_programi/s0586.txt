Generalisations/Key Concepts  
Generalisations
The target concept of this theme ''life in the universe and the future with its components such as future 
lifestyles, future technologies and inventions'' is present in almost all languages. Students who start to learn 
a new language inevitably bring with them their pre-existing knowledge and transfer it to their new language 
learning context. For example, a student in an upper secondary school may know what ''life in the universe 
and the future with its components such as future lifestyles, future technologies and inventions'' means, but 
may not know how to say these things in a foreign language if this has not yet been covered in their existing 
curriculum. However, this existing knowledge in their own language will help them recognise, understand, 
and learn the new information more easily in a foreign language, in this case English.
Key Concepts
Life in the universe, life in the future, future lifestyles, future technologies, inventions.
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation) 
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Students will explore the theme ''Life in the Universe and the Future'' project 
competition by researching and discussing future lifestyles and future technologies and inventions. They 
will examine how advancements in artificial intelligence and robotics could impact daily life, from the use of 
robots in homes and workplaces to the development of smart cities and transportation systems. Students 
will also explore the potential of new technologies, such as renewable energy sources, space exploration, 
and medical innovations, and consider how these advancements might shape human society in the coming 
year. The assignment will require students to present their findings in a creative format, such as a report 
or presentation, imagining what life might look like in the future and how technology will redefine human 
interaction, work, and leisure. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/
or a marking scheme.
