Functions of the Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Use of the Simple Present Tense: Describing regular and extracurricular activities; describing routines, 
activities and events.
-Use of the Present Progressive Tense: Describing temporary actions or actions happening now.
-Use of The Simple Present Tense vs The Present Progressive Tense: Contrasting what usually happens with 
what is happening now.
-Use of Nothing, Something, Anything: Discussing opportunities and availability of resources at different 
contexts.
-Use of Question Tags for The Simple Present Tense & The Present Progressive Tense: Confirming or 
clarifying facts about different situations.
-Use of Adverbs of Frequency: Expressing how often certain activities or routines take place.
-Use of Questions: who, where, when, how, why, what kind of, what…like: Gathering information about 
different types of schools and their activities; exploring student experiences, facilities, and events.
-Use of Pronouns: Something, Anything, Nothing: Discussing emotional states and facilities related to 
schools; sharing information about school-related problems or solutions.
-Use of "Must/Can’t" for deduction: Making logical assumptions about school environments, activities, or 
students’ feelings.
-Use of Object Pronouns: Referring to students, teachers, or facilities in different school contexts.
