CONTENT FRAME  
LIFE THE UNIVERSE AND THE FUTURE
Sub-themes: ''Space organisations; plans and aspirations for the future''
Target Vocabulary in Use (with revisional vocabulary):
Vocabulary for space organisations: 
Nouns: NASA (National Aeronautics and Space Administration), space habitat, space  missions, an astronaut, 
a rocket, atmosphere, Mars, moonlight, a spaceship, space  station, sunlight, mystery, wonder, TSA (Turkish 
Space Agency).
Verbs: To be responsible for, to be involved in, to collect samples, to shine, to reflect, to survive.
Vocabulary for plans and aspirations for the future: 
Nouns: Contribution, a goal, career.
Verbs: To set goals, to pursue dreams, to accomplish, to contribute, to entertain, to improve, to achieve, to 
reach.
Phrasal verb: To come up with.
Adjectives: Bold, optimistic, realistic.
