THE ENGLISH LANGUAGE CURRICULUM
CONTENT FRAME  
CITY AND SOCIAL LIFE
Sub-themes: ''Different communities and lifestyles in the rural areas and cities; problems related to 
infrastructure and planning in the city and solutions''
Target Vocabulary in Use (with revisional vocabulary):
Vocabulary for different communities and lifestyles in the rural areas and cities : 
Nouns: Dairy products, a cultural event, a beauty centre, accommodation, childhood, a consumer, a 
countryside, a custom, financial issues, an immigrant, security, unemployment, youth, entertainment.
Verbs: To bake bread, to access, to afford, to benefit, to experience, to gather, to limit.
Phrasal verbs: To go up, to get around.
Adjectives: Central, criminal, economic, educated, far, grateful, retired, unemployed.
Adverb: Absolutely.
Vocabulary for problems related to infrastructure and planning in the city and solutions:
Nouns: A connection, lack of green spaces, high property prices, high energy demand, planning, power cuts, 
a development, an improvement, a pipe, a warning, waste.
Verbs: To improve safety, to expand (public transportation), to meet demands.
Adjectives: Alternative, unable, various.
Adverbs: Approximately, highly.
*National and religious days, festivals and celebrations: 
“29 October Republic Day”, “23 April National Sovereignty and Children’s Day”, “19 May Commemoration of

*Local Cultural, Social and  Moral Considerations of the Theme
In this theme, specific examples, cases, situations, events, and practices reflecting Turkish social, cultural, 
and moral customs, traditions, and heritage  will be included across all sub-topics. This consideration will be 
age-and-level appropriate and selected with the Turkish learners’ social and cultural context in mind.  A table 
of contents for this section is provided in the introduction to the whole English Language Curriculum.
Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
The Future Perfect Tense: (By the end of the season, we will have harvested all the vegetables from the 
farm).
Functions of the Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Use of the Future Perfect Tense: Describing actions that will be completed before a specified time in the 
future.
Target Social Language Expressions in Use: 
The race was neck and neck until the very end!
The crowd went wild when the home team scored!
Target Phonological Sounds in Use:
Vowel: i (/aı/, /ı/, /i:/)
Consonants: j (/dʒeɪ/, /dʒ/); k: (/k/); l: (/ɛl/; /l/)
*(Phonetic symbols will not be taught. Only sounds will be practised through the target words in the theme.)
