Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Conditional 3: (If the space agency had received more funding, they would have been able to launch a new 
spacecraft this year).
-Mixed conditionals: (If the space tourism industry had made faster progress, commercial space flights 
would be more accessible today). 
Functions of the Grammatical Structures in Use: 
-Use of the conditional 3: Expressing hypothetical or unreal situations in the past.
-Use of mixed conditionals: Expressing regrets about past decisions or criticism of past actions.

Generalisations/Key Concepts  
Generalisations
The target concept of this theme ''life in the universe and the future with its components such as space 
organisations; plans and aspirations for the future'' is present in almost all languages. Students who start 
to learn a new language inevitably bring with them their pre-existing knowledge and transfer it to their 
new language learning context. For example, a student in an upper secondary school may know what ''life 
in the universe and the future with its components such as space organisations; plans and aspirations for 
the future'' means, but may not know how to say these things in a foreign language if this has not yet been 
covered in their existing curriculum. However, this existing knowledge in their own language will help them 
recognise, understand, and learn the new information more easily in a foreign language, in this case English.
Key Concepts
Life, life in the universe, future, life in the future, space, space organisations; plans, plans and aspirations for 
the future.
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation) 
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Students will explore the theme ''Life in the Universe and the Future'' by 
investigating topics such as space organisations; and plans and aspirations for the future. They will research 
advancements made by global space organisations and examine their ambitious missions to explore space 
and understand our universe. Students will also envision their own plans and aspirations for the future, either 
on a personal level or as part of humanity’s collective progress in space exploration and technology. The 
assignment can take the form of a creative presentation, a futuristic essay, or a visual project imagining life 
on another planet, encouraging students to think critically about humanity's place in the universe and the 
role of innovation in shaping the future. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating 
scale, and/or a marking scheme.
