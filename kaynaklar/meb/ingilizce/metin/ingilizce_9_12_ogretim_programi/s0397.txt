CONTENT FRAME  
LIFE IN THE UNIVERSE & THE FUTURE
Sub-themes: ''Planets and space; life in the universe; space explorations''
Target Vocabulary in Use (with revisional vocabulary):
Vocabulary for planets and space: 
Planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune. Nouns: Universe, galaxy, Milky Way, 
solar system, satellite, a meteor, an asteroid, a comet, an orbit, to revolve, Adjective: Celestial.
Vocabulary for life in the universe: 
Nouns: Atmosphere, a robot, a creature, a space colony, Adjectives:  tiny, unknown, weird. mysterious, rare.
Vocabulary for space explorations: 
Nouns: Space travel, space mission, telescope, an astronaut, a spacecraft, a rocket, a space shuttle, a space 
capsule Verbs: to launch, to take off, to land, to collect data, to conduct experiments, to go on a spacewalk
