THE ENGLISH LANGUAGE CURRICULUM
Generalisations/Key Concepts 
Generalisations
The target concept of this theme ''stereotypes and cultural diversity in the world; gaining awareness on 
climate change'' is present in almost all languages. Students who start to learn a new language inevitably 
bring with them their pre-existing knowledge and transfer it to their new language learning context. For 
example, a student in an upper secondary school may know what ''stereotypes and cultural diversity in the 
world; gaining awareness on climate change'' means, but may not know how to say these things in a foreign 
language if this has not yet been covered in their existing curriculum. However, this existing knowledge in 
their own language will help them recognise, understand, and learn the new information more easily in a 
foreign language, in this case English.
Key Concepts
Stereotypes, cultural diversity, in the world; awareness, gaining awareness on climate change.
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation) 
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Students will explore the theme ''Life in the Cultural and Natural World'' with 
a focus on stereotypes and cultural diversity in the world and gaining awareness of climate change. They will 
create a multimedia presentation or visual project that highlights examples of cultural stereotypes and their 
impact on global understanding, alongside stories or traditions that celebrate cultural diversity. The project 
will also address the urgent issue of climate change, examining how diverse cultures around the world are 
affected by and responding to this crisis. Students will propose actionable solutions to promote cultural 
empathy and environmental sustainability, reflecting on the interconnectedness of humanity and nature. 
The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a marking scheme.  
LEARNING-TEACHING EXPERIENCES
Basic Acceptations: Preparedness/Readiness 
This theme includes the concept of ''life in the cultural and natural world with its components such as 
stereotypes and cultural diversity in the world; gaining awareness on climate change'' and it is assumed 
that it could be present in almost all languages, with some exceptions. It is assumed that a student of an 
upper secondary school in year 12 may know what stereotypes and cultural diversity in the world; gaining 
awareness on climate change mean in their own language as it is known that almost all people who start 
to learn a new language bring their pre-existing knowledge (in their own language) with them and transfer 
it to their new language learning context. However, they may not know how to say these things in a foreign 
language if this has not yet been covered in their existing curriculum. Fortunately, this existing knowledge in 
their own language can help them recognise, understand, and learn them more easily in a foreign language, 
in this case English.
As they are students of an upper secondary school in year 12, they are expected to know some basic vocabulary 
about ''life in the cultural and natural world'' in English from previous themes and/or years such as ''worldwide, 
cultural exchange, climate, universal and so on'' and also some structures such as ''Governments should 
implement stricter laws to combat climate change. They could find alternative solutions to deforestation.'' 
in English from the previous themes and/or years. These grammar items and vocabulary are revised during 
the ''Revision Programme'' at the beginning of the year and they can be used as a basis for learning the new 
vocabulary and grammatical items in the current theme, so that the new grammatical elements in the 
current theme can be learnt easily.
