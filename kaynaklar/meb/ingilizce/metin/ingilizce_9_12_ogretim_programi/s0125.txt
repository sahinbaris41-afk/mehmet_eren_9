Objective: To help students discuss family members’ jobs, work routines, and workplaces in English through 
paired reading and question-asking by using target vocabulary and target grammar.
Activity 1: Paired Jigsaw Reading – Family Jobs and Workplaces
The teacher prepares a text that covers the jobs, work routines, and workplaces of different family members. 
The text is divided into two sections, each emphasising on two different jobs (e.g., a teacher and a mechanic, 
or a nurse and a chef). Students are paired up, and each student reads one section of the text independently. 
After reading, the students take turns asking each other questions about the jobs and workplaces they didn’t 
read about. For example, Student A might ask, ''Where does the mechanic work?'' or ''What are the nurse’s 
daily routines?''. This helps them complete their understanding of the entire text by filling in the missing 
information from their peer. At the end of the activity, each pair submits a completed worksheet that includes 
details about all jobs and workplaces covered in the text.
The teacher evaluates how effectively students communicate and collaborate with their peers. S/he checks 
the correctness in answering questions and the completion of the worksheet for clarity, accuracy, and 
appropriate use of job-related vocabulary. Students reflect on their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
Objective: To talk about family members’ jobs, work routines, and workplaces in English by using target 
vocabulary and target grammar.
