and moral customs, traditions, and heritage  will be included across all sub-topics. This consideration will be 
age-and-level appropriate and selected with the Turkish learners’ social and cultural context in mind.  A table

Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-The Simple Present Tense: (Subject verb agreement with subject pronouns: I/you/ we/they/he/she/it). 
(Affirmative, negative, interrogative): (Where do you live? I live in a flat.) (What type of a house is it? It’s/is a 
cottage.)
-Time expressions for the present time: Now, at the moment, at present
-The Present Progressive Tense: (What are you doing at home now? I’m/am cleaning our house now. (Subject 
verb agreement with subject pronouns: I/you/we/they/he/she/it). (Affirmative, negative, interrogative)
-Possessive adjectives: My, our, your, his, her, its, our, their.
Functions of the Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Use of the Simple Present Tense: Asking and answering about types of houses (What kind of a house is it? 
It’s/is a cottage.) Asking and answering about where people live in terms of house types. 
-Use of the Present Progressive Tense: Describing present activities in the house. What is s/he doing now? 
S/he’s/is having breakfast in the kitchen now.)

Generalisations/Key Concepts  
Generalisations
The concept of ''types of houses, rooms, furniture, and activities in the house'' is present in all languages 
because it is a fact that almost all people, with some exceptions, have some types of houses, rooms, 
furniture, and activities in their houses. The majority of people talk about their or others’ houses, rooms, 
furniture and activities that take place in their house to share information about themselves and/or others in 
class, at home, or in other places. Engaging in such conversations allows them to describe themselves, the 
types of their houses and rooms, furniture, and activities in their houses.
Key Concepts
Types of houses, types of rooms, types of furniture for different parts of the house, activities that can be 
done in the house.
