Objective: To recognise family traditions, values, and customs in English by using target vocabulary.
Activity: Match the Moments
The teacher creates a matching activity where students match pictures with family-related vocabulary and 
concepts. For example, one column shows pictures such as a family celebrating a birthday, a messy room, 
a family watching TV, or someone setting a table, while the other column includes words or phrases like 
''celebrating birthdays'', ''mess'', ''family meals'', and ''streaming service''. Students match the pictures with the 
correct word by drawing lines. After completing the activity, the teacher checks the answers as a class, 
discusses the significance of each image, and provides feedback on students’ understanding, pronunciation, 
and participation. Students reflect on their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
