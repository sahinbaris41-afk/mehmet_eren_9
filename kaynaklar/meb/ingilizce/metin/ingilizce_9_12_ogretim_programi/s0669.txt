Objective: To describe positive and negative effects of technology on family life in English by using target 
vocabulary.
Activity 1: Grouping the Effects of Technology on Family Life
The teacher prepares a reading text for this activity. Students read the text below or another text and underline 
the positive and negative effects of technology on family life. After reading, they group these effects under 
two headings: ''Positive Effects'' and ''Negative Effects''. Finally, each student shares one positive and one 
negative effect with the class. In the end, the teacher provides constructive feedback on the content and 
use of vocabulary.
Activity Text: Technology, such as smartphones, video calls, and social media, helps families stay in touch, 
work, and study online, making life convenient and timesaving. It also allows families to monitor health 
and track kids. However, it can reduce face-to-face interaction, cause distractions, and lead to issues like 
sleep problems, technology addiction, and anxiety. To avoid these problems, families can set boundaries, 
play board games, or do outdoor activities. By using technology responsibly, families can strengthen bonds 
and connect with the community, sharing values and building unity. Students reflect on their thoughts and 
feelings about the activity.
