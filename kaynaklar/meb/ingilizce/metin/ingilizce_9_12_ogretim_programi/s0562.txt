LIFE IN NATURE & GLOBAL PROBLEMS
Sub-themes: ''Globalisation, problems and solutions; environmental issues and solutions''
Target Vocabulary in Use (with revisional vocabulary):
Vocabulary for globalisation, problems and solutions:
Nouns: A cultural exchange, global issues, a standard of living, cultural diversity, job loss,
policy, anti-globalisation, health crisis, consumption habits.
Verbs: To trade, to suffer, to supply, to regulate.
Phrasal verbs: To face up to, to come up with.
Adjectives: Worldwide, cross-cultural.
Adverbs: Worldwide.
Vocabulary for environmental issues and solutions:
Nouns: Glacier, a waterfall, ozone layer, ecosystem, contamination, ice caps, sea level rise, an earthquake, 
global heating, a windstorm, a tornado, zero-waste, greenhouse effects, carbon emissions.
Verbs: To release, to consume, to contribute, to capture.
Phrasal verbs: To cut down on, to clean up.
Adjectives: Nuclear, poisonous
Adverbs: Wisely.
