1.5. STRUCTURE OF THE ENGLISH LANGUAGE CURRICULUM
The English Language Curriculum has a theme-based foundation and has been designed to be applied in 
these programmes: Primary Education, Years 2, 3, 4; Lower Secondary Education, Years 5, 6, 7, 8; Upper 
Secondary Education, Years 9, 10, 11, 12; Multilingual Language Programme, Lower Secondary Education, 
Years 5, 6, 7, 8; Upper Secondary Education with Preparatory Year and Years 9, 10, 11, 12. The thematic 
sequence of the English Language Curriculum is based on 8 main themes and each main theme is divided into 
at least three or two sub-themes and the content details are constructed accordingly. The structure of the 
themes and the explanations related to this structure are presented below in a systematic nature.
