ENG.11.3.S5.
RECONSTRUCTING/COMMUNICATING THROUGH SPEAKING: Collaborative and communicative activities 
are designed and used to revise and consolidate the whole theme on “providing well-being with physical 
health (body and illnesses); providing well-being with mental and emotional health” (V4.5, LS5, CS2.13, 
CS2.14). Storification, drama, and gamification techniques are actively and extensively used for further 
speaking practice throughout the theme (SELS2.1, SELS2.2, V4.1, V4.2, LS4, LS9). Students are encouraged 
to produce several spoken products related to the extended contexts such as dialogues, speeches, short 
texts, presentations, etc. to interact and to communicate with peers and/or other people inside and/or 
outside the school by using the language including the target words, language chunks, and social language 
expressions for the current theme (SELS2.3, V3.4, V7.1, V7.2, LS1, CS2.20). AI-based technologies can be 
used for various activities, giving students access to authentic materials. They can also produce digital 
materials and projects for speaking at school and at home through recordings (D3.3, SELS3.1, SELS3.2, 
V7.3, V11.1, LS2). All these speaking activities can be evaluated as described in items (1), (2), (3), (4), (5), (7), (8), 
(9), (10), and (11) in the section “1.2. Principles for the Implementation of the English Language Curriculum”.
ENG.11.3.S6.
REFLECTING THROUGH SPEAKING: Students are encouraged to reflect individually or collaboratively in 
groups on their own individual learning (SELS1.3) or on their collaborative learning in pairs, groups or as a 
whole class collaboratively (D2.3, SELS2.2, CS2.15). The activities will help and support students in these 
feedback and reflection sessions. Their feedback is shared with peers and the teacher and takes the form 
of interactive and ongoing feedback that can help their own improvement (SELS1.2, SELS2.1) and also they 
contribute to the improvement of their peers in their learning processes (V4.1, V4.3, V12.1, V12.3, LS1).
DIFFERENTIATION
Expansion
Objective: To create and share future plans for physical and mental well-being in English, through their 
speaking and writing skills by using target vocabulary.
Activity 1: Future Well-being Plan
Students create a future plan for their well-being, focusing on physical and mental health and sustainability. 
They use target vocabulary such as ''balanced diet'', ''adequate sleep'', ''to manage'', ''to exercise'', and ''to set 
goals''. For example, a student might write, ''In the future, I will eat a balanced diet and do yoga to stay strong, 
and I will practice mindfulness to feel calm''. After writing their plans, students share them in small groups 
and compare their ideas using sentences like, ''My plan is more relaxing than my current habits'', or ''It will 
be as healthy as my diet now but more fun''. The teacher gives feedback on vocabulary use, grammar, and 
pronunciation. Students reflect on their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
Supporting
Objective: To discuss problems and provide supportive advice in a context in English through a role play 
activity by using the target vocabulary.
Activity 1: Friendly Advice Chat
Students work in pairs as two friends having a conversation about their problems. The teacher prepares 
papers with symptoms and related advice (e.g., ''low energy—get adequate sleep'') and places them in a box.

because I have low energy''.), while Friend B listens and offers supportive advice based on the paper (e.g., ''You
