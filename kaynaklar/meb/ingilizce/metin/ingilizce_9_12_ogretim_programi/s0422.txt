Generalisations/Key Concepts  
Generalisations
The target concept of this theme ''school life and education with its components such as comparing different 
types of schools; comparing different education systems; national days and celebrations'' is present 
in almost all languages. Students who start to learn a new language inevitably bring with them their pre-
existing knowledge and transfer it to their new language learning context. For example, a student in an 
upper secondary school may know what ''school life and education with its components such as comparing 
different types of schools; comparing different education systems; national days and celebrations'' means, 
but may not know how to say these things in another language if this has not yet been covered in their existing 
curriculum. However, this existing knowledge in their own language will help them recognise, understand, 
and learn the new information more easily in a foreign language, in this case English.
Key Concepts
School life, education, comparing different types of schools, comparing different education systems, 
national days, celebrations.
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation) 
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Students will create a project titled "School Life and Education Around the 
World, focusing on the sub-themes of comparing different types of schools, education systems, and national 
days or celebrations related to education. Students will begin by researching and comparing different 
types of schools, such as public, private, and vocational institutions, highlighting their unique features and 
approaches to education. They will explore and compare education systems in various countries, discussing 
similarities and differences in teaching methods, curriculum structures, and student outcomes. Additionally, 
students will examine how national days and celebrations, such as Republic Day, Teacher’s Day or Education 
Week, are observed in different cultures, reflecting on their importance in promoting education and school 
pride. The project will include written content, visuals, and personal reflections, and students will present 
their findings in class. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a 
marking scheme.

different types of schools; comparing different education systems; national days and celebrations'' and it is 
assumed that it could be present in almost all languages, with some exceptions. It is assumed that a student 
of an upper secondary school in year 11 may know what school life and education, different types of schools; 
comparing different education systems; national days and celebrations mean in their own language as it is 
known that almost all people who start to learn a new language bring their pre-existing knowledge (in their 
own language) with them and transfer it to their new language learning context. However, they may not know 
how to say these things in another language if this has not yet been covered in their existing curriculum. 
Fortunately, this existing knowledge in their own language can help them recognise, understand, and learn 
them more easily in a foreign language, in this case English.
