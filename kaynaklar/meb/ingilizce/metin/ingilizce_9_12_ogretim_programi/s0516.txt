CONTENT FRAME  
LIFE IN THE NEIGHBOURHOOD, CITY & SOCIAL LIFE
Sub-themes: ''Types of cities, social life in the rural areas and cities''
Target Vocabulary in Use (with revisional vocabulary):
Vocabulary for types of cities:
Nouns: A neighbourhood, an eco-friendly city, a metropolis, a mega city, a municipality, a sustainable city, an 
industrial city, a suburb, a slow city.
Adjectives: Historic, cultural, liveable, walkable, like-minded, well-planned, urban.
Vocabulary for social life in the rural areas and cities:
Nouns: Exhibitions, concerts, recreational activities, residents, bonds.
Verbs: To socialise.
Phrasal verbs: To come across, to meet up.
Adjectives: Peaceful, limited, long-lasting, overpopulated, cosy, sincere.
Adverbs: Close.
