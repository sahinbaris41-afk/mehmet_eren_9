REFLECTING THROUGH SPEAKING: Students are encouraged to reflect individually or collaboratively in 
groups on their own individual learning (SELS1.3) or on their collaborative learning in pairs, groups or as a

DIFFERENTIATION
Expansion
Objective: To talk about global problems related to natural resources and solutions by using the target 
vocabulary. 
Activity 1: Solutions for Global Problems
The teacher divides students into small groups and assigns each group a global problem, such as climate 
change or water shortage. Students discuss the problem using the target vocabulary, including terms such 
as ''renewable resources'', ''efficient energy use'', ''to recycle'', and ''to raise awareness'', as well as structures 
such as ''which, that, and so''. Each group creates a plan that explains the problem, its causes, and solutions 
with reasons. For example, for climate change, a group might state: ''Climate change, which is caused by 
greenhouse gases and overuse of fossil fuels, requires immediate action. We suggest using renewable 
resources, such as solar and wind energy, that are sustainable and reduce carbon emissions. To solve this, it 
is important to raise awareness, so people learn to save energy and recycle''. After each group presentation, 
the teacher provides feedback on correct use of target vocabulary and target grammar. Students reflect on 
their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
Supporting
Objective: To identify and categorise natural resources, global problems, and actions to protect the 
environment in English by using the target vocabulary.
Activity 1: Save the Planet: Match and Share
The teacher shows flashcards of natural resources such as water, forests, and animals, asks students 
to name them, and repeats the words. Next, s/he provides a worksheet with vocabulary words like ''solar 
energy'', ''pollution'', and ''to recycle'' and asks students to match them to the categories ''Natural Resources'', 
''Global Problems'', and ''Protecting Natural Resources'' by drawing lines. For example, students will match

Resources''. After completing the worksheet, students take turns reading aloud the words they matched in
