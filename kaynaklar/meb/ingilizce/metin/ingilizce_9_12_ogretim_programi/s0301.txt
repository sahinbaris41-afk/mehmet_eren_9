CONTENT FRAME  
FAMILY LIFE & HOME
Sub-themes: ''Family members and their daily routines today and in the past; hobbies and interests of family 
members (sports & films); different types of houses and families from the world''
Target Vocabulary in Use (with revisional vocabulary): 
Vocabulary for family members and their daily routines:
Nouns: (revisional) a great-grandmother/father, a mother / father /brother /sister / daughter /son-in-law, a 
half-sister/brother, (target) a sibling, a distant relative.  Verbs: To run errands, to commute, to do household 
chores, to do the dusting, to do the laundry, to set the table, to empty the dishwasher, to do the ironing.
Types of families: 
Nouns: A single-parent family, an immediate family, a family, a nuclear family.
Vocabulary for different types of houses and families from the world: 
Types of houses: 
Nouns: (revisional) a detached house, a semi-detached house, a cottage, villa, a terraced house, (target) an 
attached house, an igloo, a hut, a houseboat, a farmhouse, a treehouse.
Vocabulary for hobbies and interests of family members (sports & films): 
Sports: 
Nouns: Playing badminton, to go cycling / running / jogging / hiking / trekking / rafting / swimming / surfing / 
skating / skiing / snowboarding, to ride a horse, to do archery, to do extreme sports.
Vocabulary for film genres: 
Nouns: Science fiction (sci-fi), thrillers, horror films, documentaries, (target) romantic comedies, sitcoms.

*Local Cultural, Social and  Moral Considerations of the Theme
In this theme, specific examples, cases, situations, events, and practices reflecting Turkish social, cultural, 
and moral customs, traditions, and heritage  will be included across all sub-topics. This consideration will be 
age-and-level appropriate and selected with the Turkish learners’ social and cultural context in mind.  A table 
of contents for this section is provided in the introduction to the whole English Language Curriculum.
Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-The Simple Past Tense (to be: Was/Were, There was/There were) (Regular, Irregular verbs): (Yesterday, there 
was a big family gathering at our detached house. My sisters were very hardworking; they prepared meals, 
tidied up, and set the table every day. My brother played basketball every weekend when he was younger.)
-Question tags: (Your sibling likes watching science fiction films as a teenager, doesn’t she?)
-Could for ability: (My sister could ride a horse very well when she was younger. My father could do archery 
when he was a teenager living in the countryside.)
-Used to: (When I was a child, we used to live in a small cottage in the mountains. My great-grandmother used 
to wake up at 5 a.m. every day to do the cleaning and washing up.)
Functions of the Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Use of the Simple Past Tense (to be: was/were, there was/were)
Regular, irregular verbs: Describing actions, states and/or events that happened and were completed in the past.
