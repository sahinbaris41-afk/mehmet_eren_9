ENG.10.5.S6. Students can reflect on their experiences, thoughts, ideas, and feelings related to the speaking 
process about the current content on ''life in the neighbourhood, city and social life with activities to do 
in attractions (cafes, cinemas; gyms, sports clubs); getting help from services (pharmacies, hospitals, 
etc.); pros and cons of different types of transportation in the neighbourhood and city'' in relation to 
themselves or others, both individually and/or with others.
a) Students reveal their knowledge, experiences, thoughts, and feelings that they review critically in 
relation to the current speaking-expression content and process.
b) Students share their personal knowledge, experiences, thoughts, and feelings that they critically 
review in relation to the current speaking-expression content and process with others.
c) Students produce new verbal and written work after participating in reflective activities related to the 
current speaking-expression content and process.
CONTENT FRAME  
LIFE IN THE NEIGHBOURHOOD & CITY & SOCIAL LIFE
Sub-themes: ''Activities to do in attractions (cafes, cinemas; gyms, sports clubs); getting help from services 
(pharmacies, hospitals, etc.); pros and cons of different types of transportation in the neighbourhood and 
city''
Target Vocabulary in Use (with revisional vocabulary): 
Transportation: A bus, a train, a bike / bicycle, a taxi, on foot, a car, an aeroplane (air-plane-AmE).
Vocabulary for activities to do in attractions and recreational places in the neighbourhood and city (cafes, 
cinemas; gyms, sports clubs): 
Verbs: To surf the Net, to chill out, to buy popcorn/ snacks, to attend fitness classes, to join tournaments, 
to do Pilates.
Vocabulary for getting help from services in the neighbourhood and the city (pharmacies, hospitals etc):
Nouns: A first-aid kit, a bandage. Verbs: To buy medicine, to ask for advice, to buy health products/ vitamins, 
to check blood pressure, to attend check-ups, to browse (in a chemist’s)
Vocabulary for pros and cons of different types of transportation in the neighbourhood and city:
Transportation: Nouns: Public transport, an underground (subway- AmE), a ferry, a motorbike, a tram, a ship, 
a lorry (truck-AmE), e-scooters.
Pros and cons: Adjectives: Eco-friendly, reliable, efficient, affordable, convenient, time-saving, time-
consuming, polluting, unsafe, uncomfortable, (prone to) delays.
Target Grammatical Structures  in Use:
-The Past Progressive Tense: (I was going to the school by bike yesterday morning. I was going to school on 
foot while you were taking the bus last morning.)
-The Simple Past Tense vs Past Progressive Tense: (The doctor was checking my blood pressure when you 
called me.)
-Question tags: (You came to school on foot yesterday, didn’t you? There wasn’t a traffic jam two hours ago, 
was there?)
