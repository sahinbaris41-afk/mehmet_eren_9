CONTENT FRAME
PERSONAL LIFE & WELL-BEING
Sub-themes: "Body parts, human illnesses and diseases; human nature and human well-being today and in 
the future; fashion and personal fashion preferences''
Target Vocabulary in Use (with revisional vocabulary):
Vocabulary for body parts: 
Nouns: A forehead, an eyebrow, an eyelash, a chin, a chest, lips, a tongue.
Vocabulary for human illnesses and diseases:
Nouns: A cold, flu, a fever, a cough, a headache, a stomach ache, a sore throat, a toothache, a backache, an 
earache, an allergy, an allergic reaction, diabetes, COVID, acne, malaria, chickenpox, measles, a painkiller, an 
emergency. Verbs: To vomit, to feel dizzy, to breathe.
Adjectives: Painful.
Vocabulary for human nature and human well-being today and in the future:  
Nouns: Empathy, social connection, happiness, global citizenship, digital detox, sustainability, self-care. 
Adjectives: Ambitious, compassionate, courageous.
Vocabulary for fashion and personal fashion preferences:
Nouns: (Accessories): A scarf, a headscarf, a necklace, earrings, a bracelet, a ring, a watch, a belt, a hat, 
a handbag, a hairband (a headband-AmE),  a cap, a benie, a tie, a bow tie, gloves, a backpack (rucksack). 
Adjectives: (Styles): Stylish, trendy, classy, smart casual, vintage, neat, well-dressed, (Clothing materials): 
denim, leather, cotton. (Clothing patterns): floral, striped, plain,  checked, patterned.

*Local Cultural, Social and  Moral Considerations of the Theme
In this theme, specific examples, cases, situations, events, and practices reflecting Turkish social, cultural, 
and moral customs, traditions, and heritage  will be included across all sub-topics. This consideration will be 
age-and-level appropriate and selected with the Turkish learners’ social and cultural context in mind.  A table 
of contents for this section is provided in the introduction to the whole English Language Curriculum.
Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-All future tenses mixed: (revisional): (By next year, she will have improved her health by running every day. 
She is going to start yoga this weekend to improve flexibility and balance. I will try to stay resilient no matter 
how difficult things get in the future.)
-Can for ability (revisional): (I can lift heavy weights with my arms because I work out every day.)
-Comparative Adjectives (Regular, Irregular) (revisional): (His new haircut looks better than his previous style. 
She is more ambitious than anyone in her social circle.)
-The Simple Present vs Present Progressive for Future: (She exercises every morning to keep fit and healthy. 
She is exercising at the gym right now to stay fit.)
-Should for Advice: (You should wear a scarf in winter to protect your neck from cold winds.)
-Could for ability: (She could run a marathon if she trains her legs and increases her endurance.)
-Adjectives (regular, -ing ending, -ed ending): (She has a beautiful face with sparkling eyes. The patient felt 
dizzy after experiencing a high fever. Her amazing energy inspires everyone in her fashion design team. After 
running for an hour, he felt exhausted and his legs were sore).
-As…as, same…as, like, alike: (My sister’s hair is as long as mine, both silky and healthy. Her style is similar to 
a vintage fashion icon, effortlessly chic.)
