“Blended Learning” has been taken as the second conceptual and theoretical framework for the use of 
technology in the English Language Curriculum since it combines up-to-date online and face-to-face teaching 
methods with technological tools, media, and techniques. In terms of the amount of technology and the 
types of techniques, ways, and means of the technology integration into the learning- teaching process in 
all years. In the English Language Curriculum, the use of technology refers to the integration of technology 
into the materials, teaching and learning process of lessons inside and outside the classroom for facilitating, 
enriching, and expanding learning, using and communicating in English. Theoretically, it is derived from 
“Active Learning” which aims to use the technology to help learners think and learn more deeply by facilitating 
their learning process in an enjoyable way, in appropriate contexts through technological means by using 
real, natural, and authentic language.
Incorporating the Cultural and Moral Foundations of the Century of Türkiye Education Model into 
the English Language Curriculum  
In line with the Century of Türkiye Education Model, the English Language Curriculum gives importance to 
enabling students to develop sensitivity towards national and spiritual values and to recognise their own 
cultural identities. These aspects have been given particular attention in the curriculum. In designing the 
sub-themes and the contexts within these themes, care has been taken to ensure that the content aligns 
with students’ cultural identities and these core values. To this end, the programme incorporates activities 
related to sports, arts, literature along with moral values and traditions, national and religious days, 
celebrations, and events.
This approach aims to both strengthen students’ connection with their own national, cultural, and global 
worlds and enable them to express their national and cultural elements while learning English. In this way, 
the English Language Curriculum demonstrates consistency with the national, cultural, and pedagogical 
framework of the Century of Türkiye Education Curriculum model. In this line, some examples to the national, 
religious, traditional and cultural celebrations and days are presented below.
