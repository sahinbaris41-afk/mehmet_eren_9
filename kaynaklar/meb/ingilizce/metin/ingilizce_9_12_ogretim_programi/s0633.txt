Generalisations/Key Concepts  
Generalisations
The target concept of this theme ''personal life and well-being with its components such as providing personal 
well-being through social health; providing personal well-being with economic stability (fashion addicts, 
shopaholics)'' is present in almost all languages. Students who start to learn a new language inevitably bring 
with them their pre-existing knowledge and transfer it to their new language learning context. For example, a 
student in an upper secondary school may know what ''personal life and well-being with its components such 
as providing personal well-being through social health; providing personal well-being with economic stability 
(fashion addicts, shopaholics)'' mean, but may not know how to say these things in a foreign language if this has 
not yet been covered in their existing curriculum. However, this existing knowledge in their own language will help 
them recognise, understand, and learn the new information more easily in a foreign language, in this case English.
Key Concepts
Personal life, well-being, personal well-being, social health, providing personal well-being, providing personal 
well-being with economic stability, fashion addicts,  shopaholics.
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation) 
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Students will explore the theme ''Personal life and well-being'' with a focus 
on the sub-themes of providing personal well-being through social health and providing personal well-being 
with economic stability, including topics such as fashion addiction and shopaholics. Students will prepare 
a research-based presentation that examines how maintaining strong social connections contributes to 
emotional and mental health. They will also analyse the economic aspects of personal well-being, discussing 
the impact of shopping or excessive spending on fashion, individual stability and societal norms. The project 
should include practical strategies for achieving a balanced lifestyle, such as budgeting techniques, fostering 
meaningful relationships, and setting healthy boundaries with consumer habits. Students will conclude their 
assignment by proposing actionable steps for maintaining personal well-being in a socially and economically 
sustainable way. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a marking 
scheme.
