REFLECTING THROUGH SPEAKING: Students are encouraged to reflect individually or collaboratively in 
groups on their own individual learning (SELS1.3) or on their collaborative learning in pairs, groups or as a 
whole class collaboratively (D2.3, SELS2.2, CS2.15). The activities will help and support students in these 
feedback and reflection sessions. Their feedback is shared with peers and the teacher and takes the form 
of interactive and ongoing feedback that can help their own improvement (SELS1.2, SELS2.1) and also they 
contribute to the improvement of their peers in their learning processes (V4.1, V4.3, V12.1, V12.3, LS1).
DIFFERENTIATION
Expansion
Objective: To talk about cultural celebrations in the world, sports and sports cultures and competitions in 
English by using target vocabulary.
Activity 1: Global Music and Sports Highlights
The teacher starts by showing clips of famous music festivals (e.g., Salzburg Festival, Glastonbury) and sports 
competitions (e.g., FIFA World Cup, Wimbledon) and asks students to discuss where they take place and 
what makes them special. The teacher introduces target vocabulary such as ''orchestra'', ''anthem'', ''athlete'', 
''stadium'', and ''championship'' with example sentences using defining and non-defining relative clauses 
(e.g., ''Wimbledon, which takes place in England, is famous for tennis''). Students then choose one festival 
or competition, write sentences describing it, and explain why it is important using connecting words such 
as ''therefore'' or ''thus'' (e.g., ''This event is popular worldwide; therefore, it attracts many visitors''.). Finally, 
students present their work to the class. The teacher gives feedback on the accuracy of their sentences and 
pronunciation. Students reflect on their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
Supporting
Objective: To talk about cultural celebrations in the world; sports and sports cultures and competitions in 
English by using target vocabulary.
Activity 1: Music and Sports Around the World
The teacher starts the lesson by showing pictures of famous music festivals and sports events (e.g., FIFA 
World Cup, Olympics) and asks students if they know them, where they are held, or if they have seen them 
before. Then, the teacher introduces simple words like ''orchestra'', ''anthem'', ''parade'', ''stadium'', ''athlete'', 
and ''championship'' with example sentences such as ''Orange Flower Carnival is held in Adana, in Türkiye, and 
it has colourful parades''. Students pick a music festival or sports event, write one or two sentences about it, 
and draw a picture. Finally, they show their drawings to the class, explain where the event happens and why 
they chose it, while the teacher gives feedback on their sentences and pronunciation. Students reflect on 
their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
