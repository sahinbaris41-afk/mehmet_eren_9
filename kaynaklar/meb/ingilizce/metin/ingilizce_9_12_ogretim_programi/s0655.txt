CONTENT FRAME  
FAMILY LIFE AND HOME
Sub-themes: ''The impact of technology on family life; the relationships between the family and community''
Target Vocabulary in Use (with revisional vocabulary): 
Vocabulary for the impact of technology on family life:
Terms for technology: 
Nouns: Streaming services, privacy settings, a relative, lack of attention, generation gap, access, wearable 
devices, lack of interaction, anxiety, depression, sleeping disorder, technology addiction, obesity, work-life 
balance.
Verbs: To monitor health, to do outdoor activities, to educate (family members), to apologise.
Adjectives: Remote, convenient, efficient.
Adverbs: Particularly, mainly.
Vocabulary for the relationships between the family and community:
Nouns: An identity, unity, a bond.
Verbs: To share values, to participate, to strengthen ties, to overcome barriers.
Adjectives: Emotional (well-being)
