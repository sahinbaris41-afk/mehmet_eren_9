Activity 2: Reconstructing the Film Schema
In this activity, students work in groups and are provided with different parts of a text describing a futuristic 
film (e.g., the plot, characters, and futuristic elements such as space exploration or advanced technology). 
Each group member receives a unique section of the text to read individually. One student from the group 
stands at the board to create a film schema, including elements like the title, main plot, characters, and key 
futuristic themes.
Group members, after reading their parts, explain the information to the student at the board in their own 
words, ensuring they use the target vocabulary such as futuristic, advanced, technological and the target 
grammar where appropriate (e.g., ''The film will focus on robots saving humanity''.2). The student at the board 
uses the shared information to fill in the schema.
In the end, the teacher reveals the original film schema, and the class compares it to the reconstructed 
version. The groups discuss the differences and provide feedback on the accuracy and clarity of their 
communication. The teacher and peers give constructive feedback, highlighting effective explanations, 
use of vocabulary, and teamwork, while offering suggestions for improvement. This activity ensures active 
participation and collaborative learning while fostering critical thinking and language development. Students 
reflect on their thoughts and feelings about the activity.

Objective: To help students describe different film genres in English by using the target vocabulary and 
target grammar.
Activity 1: Film Genre Match-Up
In this activity, students receive a worksheet containing film genre names such as comedy, action, time 
travel, and space adventure alongside images representing each genre. They match each image with the 
correct genre name, helping them associate vocabulary with visual cues. To reinforce support and enhance 
their understanding, students write one sentence about their favourite genre by using target language items, 
for example, ''Comedy films make people laugh''. A word bank with genre names and sample sentences is 
provided to guide them. At the end of the activity, students actively share their sentences with the class or 
in small groups, fostering engagement and allowing them to practice the target vocabulary. After sharing, 
the teacher and peers provide feedback, focusing on sentence accuracy, use of target vocabulary, and 
creativity. Positive reinforcement is used to encourage participation and highlight successful language use, 
helping students to build confidence and improve their skills. Students reflect on their thoughts and feelings 
about the activity.
Objective: To talk about futuristic ideas in English by using the target vocabulary and target grammar.
Activity 2:  Futuristic Sentence Builder
In this activity, students are provided with sentence fragments on physical cards (e.g., ''Robots will'', ''help 
people'', ''in the future'') or digital cards using web 2.0 tools if available. Students rearrange the fragments 
to create meaningful and grammatically correct sentences, focusing on futuristic ideas. For those without 
access to web 2.0 tools, teachers can distribute printed sentence fragments or write them on the board for 
group work. Visual aids such as printed or drawn pictures of robots, space exploration, and alien invasions 
can be used to stimulate ideas.
After students create their sentences, they read them aloud to the class or in small groups. The teacher and 
peers provide constructive feedback, focusing on accuracy, creativity, and use of target vocabulary. Positive 
reinforcement is encouraged to build confidence and highlight successful usage of the target language. 
Students reflect on their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
