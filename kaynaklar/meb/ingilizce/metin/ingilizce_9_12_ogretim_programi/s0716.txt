Expansion
Objective: To talk about climate change and cultural diversity in English by using target vocabulary.
Activity 1: A Panel Discussion
The teacher organises a group activity simulating a panel discussion where students role-play a meeting on 
either ''Climate Change Awareness'' or ''Cultural Diversity in the World''. One student acts as the moderator 
(speaker), while other group members take on roles such as an environmental activist, a cultural expert, 
or a concerned community member. The moderator asks each participant prepared or spontaneous 
questions like, ''What steps should we take to fight with climate change?'' or ''How can we promote cultural 
harmony?'' Participants respond with their ideas using target vocabulary. After the discussion, the moderator 
summarises and reports each participant's statements to the audience using reported speech, such as: ''The 
activist said, ‘We must engage communities in recycling programs’, which I reported as: The activist said we 
must engage communities in recycling programs''. This interactive setup helps students improve vocabulary, 
reported speech, and collaborative communication skills. The teacher gives feedback on the accuracy of 
their sentences and pronunciation. Students reflect on their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
Supporting
Objective: To describe cultural diversity and climate awareness in English by using target vocabulary.
Activity 1: Matching and Storytelling
The teacher prepares a matching game where students match vocabulary words such as ''prejudice'', 
''harmony'', and ''to recycle'' with their relevant pictures to build understanding. Once the matches are 
complete, students use the matched words to create a short story about either a cultural festival their family 
or community joined or an action their school took for climate change. For instance, students might write, 
''My teacher said, ‘We should build harmony in our community’, and I reported it as: My teacher said we should 
build harmony in our community''. This activity integrates matching and storytelling with reported speech. 
Then the teacher gives feedback on their sentences and pronunciation. Students reflect on their thoughts 
and feelings about the activity.
