*Local Cultural, Social and  Moral Considerations of the Theme
In this theme, specific examples, cases, situations, events, and practices reflecting Turkish social, cultural, 
and moral customs, traditions, and heritage  will be included across all sub-topics. This consideration will be 
age-and-level appropriate and selected with the Turkish learners’ social and cultural context in mind.  A table 
of contents for this section is provided in the introduction to the whole English Language Curriculum.
Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Zero Conditional: (If you go to the Moon, everything floats in space. If a planet is in orbit, it travels around a 
star.)
-Conditional 1: (If you want to explore space, you can become an astronaut.)
Functions of the Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
Use of the Zero Conditional: Describing general and/or scientific facts which are generally true.
Use of the Conditional 1: Describing a possible  future situation.
Target Social Language Expressions in Use: 
It’s a small world! 
She’s so strange, she is definitely not from this planet! 
A star is born!
This is a whole new world! 
It felt like being a man on the moon!  
Target Phonological Sounds in Use:
Consonants: x: (/ɛks/, /ks/,  /gz/); y: (/waɪ//waɪ//waɪ/, /j/, /ɪ/,  /iː/); z: /zed/ (Br), /ziː/ (AmE), /z/)

Generalisations/Key Concepts   
Generalisations
The target concept of this theme ''life in the universe & the future with its components such as planets and 
space; life in the universe; space explorations'' is present in almost all languages. Students who start to learn 
a new language inevitably bring with them their pre-existing knowledge and transfer it to their new language 
learning context. Most example, a student in an upper secondary school may know what ''life in the universe 
& the future with its components such as planets and space; life in the universe; space explorations'' means 
but may not know how to say these things in another language if this has not yet been covered in their existing 
curriculum. However, this existing knowledge in their own language will help them recognise, understand, 
and learn the new information more easily in a foreign language, in this case English.
Key Concepts
Life in the universe, life in the future, planets, space, space explorations.
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation) 
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Students create a poster which is about life in 2100. They brainstorm about 
the possibilities in the future such as robots, aliens, space colonies, space travel etc. Based on these ideas, 
students produce sentences about their future using the target grammar structures. Also, they add some 
visuals on their posters. The project could also be designed with the help of web 2.0 tools if students prefer. 
As a final stage, they present their posters in the classroom and find the common possibilities about the 
future. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a marking scheme.
