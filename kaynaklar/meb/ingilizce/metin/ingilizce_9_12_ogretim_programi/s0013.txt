1.2. PRINCIPLES FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE ENGLISH LANGUAGE CURRICULUM
• The English Language Curriculum has been structured in alignment with the “Common Document of 
the Century of Türkiye Education Model”. This document should be taken into consideration in planning 
English lessons; designing and preparing course materials; planning assessment and evaluation 
processes (both for formal and alternative and process-based assessment and evaluation), designing and 
preparing assessment and evaluation materials; designing and preparing the contents of coursebooks 
and other supplementary teaching materials. All educational activities should be planned and carried 
out to ensure that the learner profile in the Common Document of the Century of Türkiye Education 
Model is achieved.
• As the English Language Curriculum will be implemented gradually, the deficiencies and problems in the 
prior learning processes related to the existing level in English should be determined by the teachers 
in English departments of the schools. The content to address these deficiencies and problems 
should be determined in the departmental meetings at the beginning of the academic year and should 
be implemented throughout the educational process. There is a revision programme in the English
