Generalisations/Key Concepts   
Generalisations
The target concept of this theme ''life in the neighbourhood, city & social life with its components such as 
types of cities (slow cities, sustainable cities…); social life in the rural areas and cities'' is present in almost all 
languages. Students who start to learn a new language inevitably bring with them their pre-existing knowledge 
and transfer it to their new language learning context. For example, a student in an upper secondary school 
may know what ''life in the neighbourhood, city & social life with its components such as types of cities (slow 
cities, sustainable cities…); social life in the rural areas and cities'' means but may not know how to say these 
things in another language if this has not yet been covered in their existing curriculum. However, this existing 
knowledge in their own language will help them recognise, understand, and learn the new information more 
easily in a foreign language, in this case English.
Key Concepts 
Life in the neighbourhood, city and social life with its components such as types of cities (slow cities, 
sustainable cities…); social life in the rural areas and cities.
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation) 
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Students will explore the theme "life in the neighbourhood, city and social 
life" with a focus on types of cities (such as slow cities and sustainable cities) and social life in rural and urban 
areas. Students will prepare a written and visual project comparing the lifestyles, social interactions, and 
environmental approaches in different city types and rural areas. They will describe the key features of slow 
cities and sustainable cities, highlighting their benefits and challenges. Additionally, they will examine how 
social life varies between rural and urban settings, considering factors like community engagement, cultural 
events, and daily routines. The project should include examples from real-life cities or neighbourhoods and 
propose ways to improve social cohesion and sustainability in their own communities. Students will present 
their findings through a creative format, such as a digital presentation or a poster. The assignment will be 
evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a marking scheme.
LEARNING-TEACHING EXPERIENCES
Basic Acceptations: Preparedness/Readiness 
This theme includes the concept of ''life in the neighbourhood, city & social life with its components such as 
types of cities (slow cities, sustainable cities…); social life in the rural areas and cities'' and it is assumed that 
it could be present in almost all languages, with some exceptions. It is assumed that a student of an upper 
secondary school in year 11 may know what types of cities (slow cities, sustainable cities…); social life in the 
rural areas and cities mean in their own language as it is known that almost all people who start to learn a 
new language bring their pre-existing knowledge (in their own language) with them and transfer it to their 
new language learning context. However, they may not know how to say these things in another language if 
this has not yet been covered in their existing curriculum. Fortunately, this existing knowledge in their own 
language can help them recognise, understand, and learn them more easily in a foreign language, in this case 
English. 
As they are students of an upper secondary school in year 11, they are expected to know some basic vocabulary 
about ''life in the neighbourhood, city & social life'' in English from previous themes and/or years such as 
''air pollution, traffic jam, public transport, cultural, city and so on'' and also some structures such as ''What 
have you done at home since yesterday evening? I have cleaned our house.'' in English from the previous 
themes and/or years. These grammar items and vocabulary are revised during the ''Revision Programme'' at 
the beginning of the year and they can be used as a basis for learning the new vocabulary and grammatical 
items in the current theme, so that the new grammatical elements in the current theme can be learnt easily.
