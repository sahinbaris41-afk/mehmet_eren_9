THE ENGLISH LANGUAGE CURRICULUM
CONTENT FRAME  
LIFE IN THE CULTURAL AND NATURAL WORLD
Sub-themes: ''Stereotypes and cultural diversity in the world; gaining awareness on climate change''
Target Vocabulary in Use (with revisional vocabulary):
Vocabulary for stereotypes and cultural diversity in the world: 
Nouns: An immigrant, a refugee, a cultural element, a similarity, poverty, a religion, a nation, a race, a native, 
stereotype.
Verbs: To represent, to risk.
Phrasal verbs: To get on well, to back up.
Adjectives: Obvious, political, religious, native, stereotypical.
Adverbs: Similarly, originally, specifically.
Vocabulary for gaining awareness on climate change:
Nouns: Climate emergency, global heating.
Verbs: To highlight climate issues, to engage with, to educate the community, to contact local representatives, 
to support green policies, to create awareness campaigns, to reduce emissions.
Adjectives: Aware of, open-minded.

*Local Cultural, Social and  Moral Considerations of the Theme
In this theme, specific examples, cases, situations, events, and practices reflecting Turkish social, cultural, 
and moral customs, traditions, and heritage  will be included across all sub-topics. This consideration will be 
age-and-level appropriate and selected with the Turkish learners’ social and cultural context in mind.  A table 
of contents for this section is provided in the introduction to the whole English Language Curriculum.
Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Modals in the past (can/would/could/should have v3): (People could have avoided prejudicing others if they 
had taken the time to learn about different cultures).
Functions of the Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Use of modals in the past (can/would/could/should have v3): Describing possibility, probability, necessity 
and obligation in the past.
Target Social Language Expressions in Use: 
Learning about different cultures is the spice of life!
Don’t judge a book by its cover!
Cultural diversity makes the world more colourful!
The Earth is getting warmer!
 Let’s do something before it’s too late!
Target Phonological Sounds in Use:
 Vowel: o: (/əʊ/, /ɒ/)
Consonants:  m: (/ɛm/, /m/); n: (/ɛn/, /n/); p: (/piː/, /p/)
