LEARNING-TEACHING EXPERIENCES
Basic Acceptations: Preparedness/Readiness
It is generally accepted that people who start to learn a new language bring with them their pre-existing 
knowledge and transfer it to their new language learning. For instance, this theme is based on the concept 
of ''films, film genres, futuristic films with futuristic ideas, and technology'' and it is present in almost 
all languages, with some exceptions. People who start to learn a new language bring their pre-existing 
knowledge with them and transfer it to their new language learning context. For instance, a student of an 
upper-secondary school in year 9, may know what a film is, what film genres are, what kind of films the 
futuristic films could be, but will not know how to say these things in another language if this has not yet 
been covered in their existing curriculum. However, students could be able to recognise and express such 
situations in their own language, and this knowledge would prepare them to recognise the same concepts 
later in their foreign language, English. 
In terms of preparation for the current new theme, it is assumed that they have learnt some basic words 
about films or other entertaining content such as ''an actor, a film, a comedy, a drama, a romance.'' and how 
to describe their feelings about them with adjectives such as ''exciting, funny, scary, good, real, interesting, 
etc.'' and how to talk about them by using some structures  such as ''What’s/is it/this? It’s/is a film. What kind 
of a film is it? It’s/is a comedy film.  What kind of films do you like? I like comedies. Do you like dramas? No, I 
don’t.'' in English from the previous themes or years. These grammar items and vocabulary can be used as 
a basis for learning the new language grammatical items in the current theme, so that the new grammatical 
elements in the current theme can be learnt easily.
Pre-Evaluation Process 
It is expected that the physical, cognitive, affective, and social factors are appropriate and suitable for 
students to learn about ''films, film genres, futuristic films with futuristic ideas, and technology''. Therefore, 
a contextual close (gapped) test about ''films'' could be given to the students for remembering and practising 
the previously learnt items could be delivered to discuss and fill in the blanks with the required words and 
phrases in pairs. This session can help them revise all the vocabulary and grammatical items used from 
previous themes or previous years as a preparatory practice. In this way, their performance can be evaluated 
in terms of how much they remember and how much they use these items correctly to build their competence 
in the current theme. It can help the teacher decide what to emphasise or practise in order to revise 
previous knowledge and to facilitate the new vocabulary and grammatical items in the current theme (Their 
performance can be evaluated by rubrics, a rating scale, and /or a marking scheme during these practices).
Establishing Links

with futuristic ideas, and technology”, by remembering their pre-existing background knowledge about it 
(imaginatively through thinking) in their own language to make links with the current activities in the theme in

words in the current (new) concept in English. To remind them of their pre-existing background knowledge
