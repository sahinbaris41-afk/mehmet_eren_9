THE ENGLISH LANGUAGE CURRICULUM
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation)
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Students prepare a self-recording (visual, audio) at school/ at home, 
conducting an interview with classmates about ''local and international festivals which are held in the city, 
different kinds of food produced for such festivals, and local and international food culture'' and present it 
to the class and receiving feedback about it. They can also write and design the content of a ''food festival'' 
programme content and then they present and apply it as an acting out activity by inviting other students as 
visitors or food presenters to the festival in class to present different food cultures, cuisines, dishes etc. The 
assignment will be evaluated by using rubrics.
LEARNING-TEACHING EXPERIENCES
Basic Acceptations: Preparedness/Readiness 
This theme is based on the concept of ''local and international festivals which are held in the city, different 
kinds of food produced for such festivals, and local and international food culture'' in English. As they are upper 
secondary students in Year 9, they are expected to know what food is and what a local food is in their own 
language. They are also aware that almost all people, although there are exceptions, would know different 
types of food, the food types served in festivals, local and international food types and food cultures from 
different parts of the world. Students can think about and express such situations in their own languages, 
but they may not know how to say them in a foreign language if they have not been covered at all or only 
partially covered in their existing curriculum. However, this existing knowledge in their own language would 
prepare them to identify the same concepts later and help them recognise, understand, and learn the new 
information about the new concept and theme more easily in their foreign language, English.
In terms of preparation for the current new theme, it is assumed that they have learnt basic types of food 
such as ''fish, chicken, eggs, bread, milk, water, fruits (tomatoes, apples, oranges, bananas), vegetables 
(potatoes, carrots, beans), fast food (unhealthy food) (hamburgers, spaghetti, sausages)'', how to ask what 
things are by using language chunks such as ''What’s/is it/this? What are they?'' how to ask for permission or 
possibility by using language chunks such as ''Can I……?'', how to ask and answer whether some food exists 
by using ''Is there/are there…? There’s/is and There are'', how to descriptive food by using adjectives such as 
''good, bad, nice, yummy'', and how to ask and answer about the amount of food with quantity words such as 
''how much, how many, some, any, much, many'' and by using grammatical structures that practise Simple 
Present Tense and Present Progressive in English from the previous themes or years. These language items 
and vocabulary can be used as a basis for learning the new language grammar items in use in the current 
theme, making the new ones in the current theme easier to learn.
Pre-Evaluation Process 
It is expected that the physical, cognitive, affective, and social factors are appropriate and suitable for 
students to learn about ''local and international festivals which are held in the city, different kinds of food 
produced for such festivals, and local and international food culture''. A short contextual written story about 
food for remembering and practising the previously learnt items could be delivered to students to discuss 
and fill in the blanks with the required words in pairs. This session can help them revise all these vocabulary 
and language grammar items in use from previous themes or previous years as a preparatory practice. In this 
way, their performance can be evaluated in terms of how much they remember and how much these items 
are used correctly to be able to build their competence in the current theme. It can help the teacher decide 
what to emphasise or practise for revising the previous knowledge and to facilitate the new vocabulary and 
grammar items in use in the current theme.
