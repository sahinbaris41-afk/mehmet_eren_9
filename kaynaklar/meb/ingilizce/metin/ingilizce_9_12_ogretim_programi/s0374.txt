Target Vocabulary in Use (with revisional vocabulary) :
Vocabulary for natural resources: 
Nouns: Renewable resources, non-renewable resources, solar energy, wind energy, geothermal energy, 
freshwater, groundwater.
Vocabulary for global problems related to natural resources: 
Nouns: Deforestation, soil pollution, habitat destruction, global warming, climate change, landslides, floods, 
loss of biodiversity, energy crisis, water shortage, overpopulation, urbanisation, industrialisation, poverty, 
carbon footprint, greenhouse gases, environmental problems.
Vocabulary for protecting natural resources: 
Verbs: To use energy efficiently, to reduce consumption, to be environmentally conscious, to raise awareness, 
to take precautions, to reuse, to limit.

*Local Cultural, Social and  Moral Considerations of the Theme
In this theme, specific examples, cases, situations, events, and practices reflecting Turkish social, cultural, 
and moral customs, traditions, and heritage  will be included across all sub-topics. This consideration will be 
age-and-level appropriate and selected with the Turkish learners’ social and cultural context in mind.  A table 
of contents for this section is provided in the introduction to the whole English Language Curriculum.
Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Defining Relative Clauses (that, which, who): (Renewable resources which include solar and wind energy are 
necessary for sustainable development.)
-Connecting Words (so…): (People need to take precautions, so they can protect wildlife from habitat 
destruction.)
Functions of the Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Use of Defining Relative clauses (that, which, who): Referring to people, objects or thingsto provide essential 
information.
-Use of connecting words (so…): Expressing cause and effect; explaining consequences. 
Target Social Language Expressions in Use: 
People need to take care of Mother nature’s bounty! 
Our planet works like a well-oiled machine! 
We’re at the tipping point with the loss of our ecosystem! 
Let’s save our planet!  
Let’s be green thumbs and plant trees!
Target Phonological Sounds in Use:
Vowel: u: (/juː/, /ʌ/, /juː/)
Consonants:  v: (/viː/, /v/); w: (/w/)
*(Phonetic symbols will not be taught. Only sounds will be practised through the target words in the theme.)
