LIFE IN THE WORLD & CULTURE
Sub-themes: ''Cultural (music and art) celebrations in the world; sports and sports cultures and competitions''
Target Vocabulary in Use (with revisional vocabulary):
Vocabulary for cultural (music and art) celebrations in the world:
Nouns: Live performance, solo performance, folk music, art exhibitions, sculpture, a studio, chants, 
symphony, anthem, mural, ceramic, pottery, handmade, a masterpiece, a presenter, a performer, an 
orchestra, rhythm, make-up.
Verbs: To rehearse, to compose, to get inspired, to take part in.
Phrasal verbs: To carry out, to go on, to pass down, to play out.
Adjectives: Impressive, spectacular.
Vocabulary for sports and sports cultures, and competitions:
Nouns: A technique, a supporter, a stadium, a court, a net, gloves, passion, a gymnasium, a tournament, a 
league, a championship, discipline, a medal, a referee, a spectator, a helmet, a bat, a legend.
Verbs: To shoot, to block, to kick, to dive.
Phrasal verbs: To warm up, to call off.
Adjectives: Competitive, experienced, proud.

*Local Cultural, Social and  Moral Considerations of the Theme
In this theme, specific examples, cases, situations, events, and practices reflecting Turkish social, cultural, 
and moral customs, traditions, and heritage  will be included across all sub-topics. This consideration will be 
age-and-level appropriate and selected with the Turkish learners’ social and cultural context in mind.  A table 
of contents for this section is provided in the introduction to the whole English Language Curriculum.
Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Defining and Non-defining Relative Clauses (when, where, whose, why, who, that, which, that, whose, of 
which): (The parade that took place yesterday was a spectacular event filled with colourful costumes and 
music).
-Connecting words (therefore, thus): (The band played energetic music; therefore, the crowd couldn’t stop 
dancing).
Functions of the Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
-Use of the defining and non-defining relative clauses (when, where, whose, why, who, that, which, that, 
whose, of which): Giving essential or extra information about the noun they modify.
-Use of connecting words (therefore, thus): Indicating a conclusion or result based on the information given 
before.
Target Social Language Expressions in Use: 
Your performance at the concert stole the show! 
As a famous singer he is always in the spotlight! 
The sprinter has just broken the world record!
