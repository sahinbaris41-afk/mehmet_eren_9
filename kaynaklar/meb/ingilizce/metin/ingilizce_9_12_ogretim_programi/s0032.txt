DIFFERENTIATION
Expansion
Objective: To improve conversation skills on the theme ‘’school life with students 
of different countries, nationalities, and languages; capitals of their countries, 
and tourist attractions; activities in their capitals and countries; national days 
and celebrations” by using target vocabulary and grammatical structures while 
gathering and organising information from a reading passage.
Supporting
Objective: To improve and use the language about ‘’school life with students of 
different countries, nationalities, and languages; capitals of their countries, and 
tourist attractions; activities in their capitals and countries; national days and 
celebrations” in English by using the target vocabulary by matching countries with 
their capitals.
