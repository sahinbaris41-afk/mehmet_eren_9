For shopping: A vegetarian, a vegan, a cashier, a shelf, a shop (store-AmE), a trolley (a cart-AmE), a receipt, a

*Local Cultural, Social and  Moral Considerations of the Theme
In this theme, specific examples, cases, situations, events, and practices reflecting Turkish social, cultural, 
and moral customs, traditions, and heritage  will be included across all sub-topics. This consideration will be 
age-and-level appropriate and selected with the Turkish learners’ social and cultural context in mind.  A table 
of contents for this section is provided in the introduction to the whole English Language Curriculum.
Target Grammatical Structures in Use:
- The Present Perfect Tense (With how long, since, for, already, just) (How long have you been gluten-free? 
I have been gluten-free since I was sixteen.) (I have been to Ireland for six years. I have already ordered fast 
food. I have just bought some mushrooms.)
-Question Tags: (Türkiye is famous for its traditional dishes, isn’t it?) (You’ve eaten sushi before, haven’t you?)
-“Be able to” (Present, Past, Future, Perfect) (She is able to cook traditional dishes from Türkiye. They were 
able to find the shop despite the heavy rain. We will be able to buy the ingredients for the recipe tomorrow. I 
have been able to visit many countries this year.)
