Generalisations/ Key Concepts 
Generalisations
The concept of ''family members’ jobs, work routines, work activities and workplaces'' is present in all 
languages because it is a fact that all people have family members who have different jobs, work routines, 
work activities and workplaces. Majority of people talk about ''their or others’ family members’ jobs, work 
routines, work activities and workplaces'' to share information about themselves and/or others in class, at 
home, or other places. Engaging in such conversations allows them to describe themselves, their family 
members or others’ family members to learn about each other better and build up stronger relationships.
Key Concepts
Family members, jobs, work routines, work activities, workplaces.
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation) 
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Students prepare an interview, a poster/fact file, a short film etc. (paper or 
digital) about the jobs, work routines, work activities of their or their peers’ family members and present it to 
the class and receive feedback about it. The assignment will be evaluated by using rubrics.
LEARNING-TEACHING EXPERIENCES
Basic Acceptations: Preparedness/Readiness 
It is generally accepted that people who start to learn a new language bring with them their pre-existing 
knowledge and transfer it to their new language learning. For instance, this theme is based on the concept of 
''family members’ jobs, work routines, work activities and workplaces''. As students in year 9 (nine) of upper-
secondary school, they are expected to know what a family member is, what a job is, what a work routine is, 
what a work activity is and what a workplace is in their own language. They are also aware of the fact that the 
majority of people in families have jobs, work routines, work activities, and workplaces. Students can think 
about and express such facts and situations in their own languages, but they may not know how to say them 
in a foreign language if they have not been covered at all or only partially covered in their existing curriculum.
