Generalisations/Key Concepts  
Generalisations
The target concept of this theme ''cultural (music and art) celebrations in the world; sports and sports 
cultures and competitions'' is present in almost all languages. Students who start to learn a new language 
inevitably bring with them their pre-existing knowledge and transfer it to their new language learning context. 
For example, a student in an upper secondary school may know what ''cultural (music and art) celebrations 
in the world; sports and sports cultures and competitions'' means but may not know how to say these things 
in another language if this has not yet been covered in their existing curriculum. However, this existing 
knowledge in their own language will help them recognise, understand, and learn the new information more 
easily in a foreign language, in this case English.
Key Concepts
Culture, music, art, cultural (music and art) celebrations, celebrations in the world, sports, sports cultures, 
competitions.
INDICATORS FOR LEARNING (Assessment and Evaluation) 
PERFORMANCE ASSIGNMENT: Students will explore the theme ''Life in the World and Culture'' with a focus on 
cultural celebrations and sports cultures and competitions. Students will research and compare significant 
cultural celebrations from around the world, such as music festivals, art exhibitions, and traditional holidays, 
exploring their historical origins, meanings, and global impact. They will also examine sports cultures, 
highlighting the importance of various sports in different countries and regions, and analyse major sports 
competitions, such as the Olympics. The assignment will include a presentation that contrasts cultural 
celebrations and sports events, showing how they reflect the values and traditions of different societies. 
Students will also discuss how these celebrations and sports competitions promote cultural exchange, 
unity, and global understanding. Finally, they will reflect on how participating in or observing such events 
contributes to shaping identities and fostering a sense of community. The assignment will be evaluated by 
using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a marking scheme.
LEARNING-TEACHING EXPERIENCES
Basic Acceptations: Preparedness/Readiness 
This theme includes the concept of ''life in the world and culture with its components such as cultural (music 
and art) celebrations in the world; sports and sports cultures and competitions'' and it is assumed that it 
could be present in almost all languages, with some exceptions. It is assumed that a student of an upper 
secondary school in year 11 may know  what life in the world and culture,  cultural (music and art) celebrations 
in the world; sports and  sports cultures and competitions mean in their own language as it is known that 
almost all people who start to learn a new language bring their pre-existing knowledge (in their own language) 
with them and transfer it to their new language learning context. However, they may not know how to say 
these things in another language if this has not yet been covered in their existing curriculum. Fortunately, this 
existing knowledge in their own language can help them recognise, understand, and learn them more easily 
in a foreign language, in this case English. 
As they are students of an upper secondary school in year 11, they are expected to know some basic vocabulary 
about ''life in the world and culture'' in English from previous themes and/or years such as ''match, to win, to 
lose, traditional, festival'' and also some structures such as ''He is the footballer who won the fair play medal. 
This international festival which is held in the city hosts thousands of people from different countries'' in 
English from the previous themes and/or years. These grammar items and vocabulary are revised during 
the ''Revision Programme'' at the beginning of the year and they can be used as a basis for learning the new 
vocabulary and grammatical items in the current theme, so that the new grammatical elements in the 
current theme can be learnt easily.
