Generalisations/Key Concepts  
Generalisations
The target concept of this theme ''classroom life and learning with its components such as learning activities 
in the classroom; personal learning styles; learning with technology in the classroom'' is present in almost all 
languages. Students who start to learn a new language inevitably bring with them their pre-existing knowledge 
and transfer it to their new language learning context. For example, a student in an upper secondary school 
may know what ''classroom life and learning with its components such as learning activities in the classroom; 
personal learning styles; learning with technology in the classroom'' mean but may not know how to say these 
things in another language if this has not yet been covered in their existing curriculum. However, this existing 
knowledge in their own language will help them recognise, understand, and learn the new information more 
easily in a foreign language, in this case English.
Key Concepts
Classroom life, learning, learning activities in the classroom, personal learning styles; learning with technology 
in the classroom.
