pasta, dessert, soup. Adjectives: Spicy, salty, hot, sweet, sour, bitter, savoury (savory-AmE), juicy, creamy, 
tender, giant. Verbs:  To taste, a stall, a (street) vendor, to crack (eggs), to stir,
