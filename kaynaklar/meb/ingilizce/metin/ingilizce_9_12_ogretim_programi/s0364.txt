DIFFERENTIATION
Expansion
Objective: To talk about international foods, their countries of origin, and types through collaborative 
discussion and critical thinking by using the target vocabulary.
Activity : Food Detective Challenge
The teacher explains that students play a game as ''Food Detectives'' to solve a mystery about three 
international dishes. The teacher plays an audio or reads a script with clues about the foods, such as their 
country of origin, type (e.g., dessert, main course), and cultural significance. Students listen carefully and fill 
out a worksheet with the following details:
Food Name:
Country of Origin:
Type of Dish (Dessert/Main Course/Snack):
Next, they work in groups to compare answers. Then, each group creates a new clue for a different food 
and exchanges it with another group to solve. The teacher gives constructive and supportive feedback on 
accuracy and pronunciation. Students reflect on their thoughts and feelings about the activity.
Supporting
Objective: To talk about popular international food items and their countries of origin in English by using 
target vocabulary.
Activity : Exploring Food Cultures Around the World
The teacher begins the lesson by showing students pictures of popular international foods (e.g., pizza, sushi, 
baklava, tacos, croissants) and asking if they know where these foods come from or if they have tried them. 
The teacher provides a list of foods and their countries of origin, then writes example sentences on the 
board, such as ''Pizza is from Italy'', or ''Baklava is a traditional Turkish dessert''. Students write their own 
sentences following this structure. Each student then chooses a favourite food from another culture, draws 
it, and labels it with a sentence such as ''Tacos are from Mexico''. Finally, students present their drawings to 
the class, explaining what the food is, where it is from, and why they like it. The teacher gives constructive 
and supportive feedback on accuracy and pronunciation. Students reflect on their thoughts and feelings 
about the activity.
