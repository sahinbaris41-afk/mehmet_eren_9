about its content. After watching it, allow

Establishing Links
Direct students’ attention to the photos 
on the double spread page. Give them a 
moment to look at them. Then ask students 
to explain what they are about and 
encourage them to share their opinions 
with the class. Guide them by asking follow-
up questions to highlight the connections 
between the photos and the topic of the 
theme. Ask them to write related words 
that they already know (learnt in previous 
years) in English on a piece of paper 
and put them in a jar or a box that you 
have prepared. Open the jar/box later, 
preferably before the vocabulary session.
