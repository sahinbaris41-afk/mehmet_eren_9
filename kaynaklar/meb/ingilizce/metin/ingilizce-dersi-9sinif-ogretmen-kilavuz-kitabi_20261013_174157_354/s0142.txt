Nature is vital to human beings’ and 
animals’ existence. It provides the air, 
water, food, and other important resources 
we need to survive. Spending time in 
nature also has significant benefits for our 
mental health, well-being, and creativity. 
That’s why we should pay attention to what 
can be done to protect nature. Of course, 
we are aware that large-scale global actions 
are crucial to reducing environmental 
threats such as deforestation, air pollution, 
and wildlife extinction. However, even small 
individual efforts can have a huge impact 
on reducing the threats to ecosystems. One 
of the most important things we can do in 
our daily lives is to minimise our average 
consumption at home and work, such as 
by buying fewer clothes, goods, gadgets, 
and food. In addition, reducing daily energy 
and water consumption has a key role in 
protecting natural resources. It reduces the 
amount of necessary fuel to produce 
electricity and the amount of release of 
greenhouse gases and other air pollutants. 
Another important action we can take is 
to upcycle old materials. It means creative 
reuse, which is the process of transforming 
waste materials or unwanted products into 
new products. Instead of throwing them 
into the rubbish that will harm wildlife, 
we can create something useful. The last
