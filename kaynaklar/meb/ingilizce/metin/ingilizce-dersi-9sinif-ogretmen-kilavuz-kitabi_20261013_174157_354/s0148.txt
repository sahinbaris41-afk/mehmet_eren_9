Digital Story 1
A bright, sunny day. Students from various countries arrive at an international school in Europe. 
Mehmet stands near the entrance and looks at a school map confusedly. Alina approaches him.
Alina: (smiling warmly) Hi there! You look a bit lost.
Mehmet: (relieved) Hello! Yes, it’s my first day at school. Let me introduce myself. I’m Mehmet.
Alina: (shaking Mehmet’s hand) Nice to meet you, Mehmet. I’m Alina.
Mehmet: Nice to meet you, too.
Alina: Where are you from?
Mehmet: I’m from Türkiye.
Alina: So, you’re Turkish?
Mehmet: Yes, I am. And you?
Alina: I’m Polish.
Mehmet: (excited) Oh, you’re from Poland! Is your native language Polish?
Alina: (smiling) Yes! And I can speak English, as you see. How about you? What languages 
can you speak fluently?
Mehmet: I can speak Turkish as my first language and I can speak English and German 
fluently, too. Well, can you show me around?
Alina: Of course. Please follow me!
They start walking together into the school.
INT. SCHOOL CORRIDORE – CONTINUOUS 
They walk along a corridor; there are other students.
Alina: (pointing ahead) The canteen is at the end of this corridor. By the way, what’s the 
capital of Türkiye?
Mehmet: Ankara.
Alina: I see. Where can tourists visit in Ankara?
Mehmet: They can visit the Ankara Castle and the Old City. It’s a popular tourist attraction.
Alina: And what can they do in Ankara?
Mehmet: They can go for a hike around Eymir Lake.
Alina: Hmm, sounds amazing!
Mehmet: (laughing) Yes, it’s really fun. What’s the capital of Poland, Alina?
Alina: It’s Warsaw. Tourists can go shopping in local markets in the Praga district.
Mehmet: And where can they visit?
Alina: They can visit the Old Town. It’s a historical site. People built it again after World War 
II.
