e  
 Ask students to work in groups of four. 
Explain that they will imagine themselves 
to be at the Cultural Exchange Fair. Remind 
groups that they should choose one of 
the countries from the unit and prepare 
short presentations similar to those in 
the audio, including tourist attractions, 
famous landmarks, etc. Encourage them 
to use phrases from the unit, focusing on 
simple language and correct pronunciation. 
As they present, remind them to speak 
clearly and confidently. Once all the groups 
have presented, encourage the class to 
ask questions to each group about the 
countries they have learnt about. Provide 
feedback on their use of the language, 
creativity in their presentations, and ability 
to engage with the audience.
