Digital Story 3
Irmak, Maya, Adrian, and Leo stop in front of a vintage shop.
Maya: (excited) This is the place I’m always telling you about! They’ve got the best 
costumes for any event.
Irmak: (smiling excitedly) I hope so! I want something different!
INT. VINTAGE SHOP – MOMENTS LATER
The group walks in, and a bell rings above them. They look around with excitement. The shopkeeper, 
an elderly woman with glasses, stands behind the counter and smiles warmly.
Shopkeeper: Hello! Are you looking for anything special today?
Irmak: Yes! We need some costumes for a special event. 
Shopkeeper: (chuckling) Oh, you’re definitely in the right place! We have costumes for every 
occasion along the back wall, with hats and accessories right next to them. 
There’s also a fitting room and a mirror at the back, so you can check how things 
look on you.
The friends nod excitedly and go in different directions to look around.
INT. VINTAGE SHOP – LATER
The friends gather in front of a large mirror.
Maya: (holding up a bright pink tutu, examining it in the mirror) What do you think of 
this, Irmak? It’s so colourful! But it’s kind of ugly, isn’t it?
Irmak: (laughing) To be honest, I wouldn’t wear that. You’d look a bit funny in it.  Maybe 
it’s not the best choice for you.
Leo: (holding up a big, floppy pirate hat) Look at this hat! (puts it on, looking unsure) 
It’s not attractive enough, right?
Irmak: I agree! You look more like a careless pirate than a brave adventurer in it.
She turns to another rail, picks up a red stylish pirate coat and shows it to Leo.
What do you think about this one, Leo?
Leo: (eyes widening) Oh, I like that! It looks great!
He puts on the coat and poses like a real pirate.
How do I look? I feel like I’m ready to sail the seas!
Maya: (smiling brightly) You look amazing! Red really suits your fair skin and blond hair. 
And you look so serious, just like a real pirate captain!
Leo: (smiling shyly) Thanks, Maya.
Irmak: (turning to Maya) What about you, Maya? Are you still thinking about the fairy 
costume?
Maya: (nods, holding up a yellow flowing dress) Yes! How about this one? I love the flow 
but does it make me look a bit too young? Is it too childish?
