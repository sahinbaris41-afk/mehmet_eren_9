Digital Story 4
A bright and spacious study room. Mia is tidying up her room, preparing her desk. She’s eagerly 
awaiting her friend, Melike, to study together. Melike  knocks on the door and enters the room, 
carrying books.
Mia: (smiling warmly) Hi, Melike! Welcome! You look wonderful!
Melike: (enthusiastically) Hi, thanks! You’ve made my day. You look amazing too.
Melike places her books on the desk, focuses on a photo, and points to it.
Melike: (continues) Your relatives?
Mia: (holding the photo, pointing at the first person in the photo) He’s my grandad, Henry; 
he lives in Edinburgh.
Mia takes a deep breath, with a hint of sadness in her eyes.
Mia: (continues) I miss him so much.
Melike: Oh, dear! Don’t you see each other often?
Mia: He’s very busy, so he can’t come here very often; he works hard and doesn’t have 
much leisure time.
Melike: I wonder what he does.
Mia: He’s an architect. He works on some new projects. He’s also in charge of developing 
new policies to protect historical sites in a state architecture firm.
Melike: (smiling) Oh, yes! You know my father is a civil engineer; architects like your grandad 
draw plans and design the structures; engineers like my father build them.
Melike points at the woman in the photo.
Melike: (continues) And is this your grandmother?
Mia: Yes, she is. Grandma Miranda. She’s a loving and caring woman.
Melike: I think she works with kids, doesn’t she?
Mia: No, she works with adults.
Melike: Where does she work?
Mia: She works as a psychologist in a clinic. She loves helping people; she counsels clients 
about their problems but nowadays, she’s thinking of retiring.
Melike: Good news for you. You’ll spend more time together.
Mia: I hope so. And the person in the middle is my aunt, Rachel.
Melike: She looks very beautiful and attractive.
Mia: Yeah, thanks. She’ll be very happy to hear your nice thoughts about her.
Melike: Well, let me guess what she works for. She probably works in the fashion or cosmetics 
sector.
Mia: (smiling) No, you never guess just by looking at her.
