Melike: Oh! You didn’t mention what Vincent does.
Mia: You won’t believe it; he lives on a farm but works as a content creator.
Melike: (astonished) Really? That’s interesting.
Mia: He loves farm animals, soil, and country life. He photographs them, publishes the 
photos online, and produces and shares information about farming.
Melike: What else does he do at the farm? Does he work alone there?
Mia: No, he helps his grandfather. He gets up very early to feed the animals, cleans the 
ground, cuts wood—something like that.
