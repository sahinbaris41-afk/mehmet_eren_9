c   Play Digital Story 5 again and ask students 
to tick the sentences that can be inferred. 
Before starting, briefly explain that 
inference means understanding something 
that is not directly stated in the text but 
can be logically concluded based on 
the given information. When they have 
finished, discuss the answers as a class to 
clarify any confusion or address possible 
misinterpretations. For stronger classes, 
you can have students explain why each 
statement can or cannot be inferred.
