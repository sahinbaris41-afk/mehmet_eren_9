Welcome, everyone! I’m your guide, Susan, 
and today, we’ll explore the beautiful 
Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus. Our 
tour includes some of the most famous 
tourist attractions on the island. But first, 
let’s start with a little introduction to the 
country.
Northern Cyprus is a fascinating place. 
Its capital is Lefkoşa, and the people here 
are mostly Turkish Cypriots. The official 
language is Turkish, but many people can 
also speak English. 
We begin our sightseeing tour here in 
Girne, a charming coastal city. If you look 
to your right, you can see Girne Castle. 
Inside the castle, there is a museum with an 
ancient shipwreck from the 4th century BCE. 
It is the only one that remains from that 
period.
Our next stop is Gazimağusa. The city 
is famous for its historical sites, such as 
the ruins of the Royal Palace and the Lala 
Mustafa Pasha Mosque. The mosque is the 
largest medieval building in the city and 
one of the most impressive structures in 
the Eastern Mediterranean. The city also 
has beautiful beaches, where you can relax 
or swim in the clear waters.
Our final stop is Lefkoşa. You can see the 
historic Venetian walls that are from the 
16th century and surround the city.
I hope you all enjoy the tour today! Please 
feel free to ask me any questions along the 
way.
