  INT. A CAFÉ – AFTERNOON
DIGITAL STORY SCRIPTSLIFE IN THE UNIVERSE & FUTURE8
Ahmet: (wisely) I enjoy historical films about past events. They impress me deeply.
Özlem: I don’t know anything about them. What do they usually focus on? Is it similar to a 
biographical film?
Ahmet: No. Film writers generally adapt novels to these films. So, they usually describe 
historical people, events, and places.
Paloma: (discontentedly) I think they seem a bit dramatic; very touching stories. I’m not brave 
enough to watch them!
Marcelo: (audaciously) Really? Are you so sensitive? Maybe you should watch only cartoon 
films.    
Özlem: (convincingly) Well, cartoon films are a great idea. I can suggest you watch an 
American animated martial arts comedy film.
Paloma: (angrily) Are you kidding? Being emotional doesn’t mean I have to watch cartoons. 
And martial arts? Oh, please!
Özlem: It’s an amazing family film. Let me tell you about the film.
Paloma looks around the café and plays with her pen, seeming uninterested in her friends.
Paloma: (sarcastically) Yes, please. I can’t wait!
Özlem: (warmly smiling) Why do you avoid watching cartoon films? I really love them. 
Paloma: (smiling) OK. I promise I’ll watch them soon. You won’t believe it but futuristic films 
are my new favourites. The stories about the future, robots, and new technology 
really interest me.
Ahmet: Definitely! I’m a big fan of these films.
Marcelo: Wait, wait. I’m excited to hear you like them.
Özlem: I love these films, too. I’m looking forward to watching a futuristic film that has great 
reviews. It’s a science fiction film with space and superhero genres.
Paloma: What is that film about? Is the future at risk in this film?
Özlem: No, they’ll save the world. The cosmos is in danger because of horrific events.
Paloma: Who are these world-saving superheroes?
Özlem: The characters are legendary superheroes. Reviews say the artificial-intelligence-
powered virtual cameras create incredible scenes. This film is a breath of fresh air.
Paloma nods and shows how curiously she listens to Özlem.
Özlem: (continues) And Marcelo, what kinds of futuristic films do you like?
Marcelo: Alien invasion and time travel. My favourite plots generally are set on future Earth. 
The Earth is under attack, and the aliens try to invade the world.
Özlem: Enough about films. What do you think the future will really be like? I really wonder 
about the future. What do you think it will be like?
Ahmet: I think robots will find effective solutions to many global issues, such as air pollution 
and climate change.
Paloma: Yes, exactly. And also, to me, scientists will develop new machines to explore space 
and people will travel to other planets.
