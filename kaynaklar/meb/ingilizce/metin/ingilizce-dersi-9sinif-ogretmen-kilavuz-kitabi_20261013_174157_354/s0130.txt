WB Audio Script 3.1
Hello, I’m Merve. Let me introduce myself 
and my family members. It’ll be difficult 
to talk about myself, but I want you to 
get to know me. I’m fifteen years old. I’ve 
been playing basketball for a long time; I’m 
much taller than my peers, which is a nice 
advantage. People in my environment say 
I’m well-built. I’m thankful for their warm 
compliment. I’m a brunette with brown 
eyes and long, wavy hair. I enjoy helping 
others, spending time with my friends, and 
doing my responsibilities on time.
This beautiful woman is my mother, Fatma. 
She really looks very young and healthy, as 
she’s 44 years old and does daily exercises 
like jogging and Pilates. She has got long, 
dark-brown, straight hair and brown 
eyes. She’s tall, slim, and beautiful. She 
always feels happy, cheerful, and full of 
energy. And she’s confident enough to give 
speeches about health to the crowds. 
My father, Hakan, is 47 years old. He’s tall 
and well-built. He’s very handsome with his 
short, straight, fair hair, and he’s got hazel 
eyes. He usually seems too serious at work 
but very funny and calm at home. He’s 
gentle and generous. 
My brother, Asil, is a lovely 12-year-old boy. 
He has got brown eyes and short, straight, 
fair hair. He’s medium-height but slim like 
me. He is sometimes too shy to make new 
friends but is mostly peaceful and quiet 
when spending time with his friends. I love 
my family very much.
