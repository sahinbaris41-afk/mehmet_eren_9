d  
 Put students into pairs and ask them to 
write a dialogue about planning a festival 
trip by considering the given prompts in 
their notebooks. Give students enough 
time to complete their dialogues and have 
them practise reading their dialogues 
aloud. Monitor the activity, walking around 
the classroom and listening to students’ 
dialogues. Offer help and feedback when 
needed. Then have pairs act out their 
dialogues in front of the class.
