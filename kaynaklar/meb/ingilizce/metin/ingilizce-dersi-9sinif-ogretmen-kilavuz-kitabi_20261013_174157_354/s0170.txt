Digital Story 8
A peaceful and spacious café. Ahmet, Marcelo, Özlem, and Paloma are sitting around a table, looking 
at the laptop screen. They’re curiously searching for the released films in cinemas. They intend to go 
to the cinema together, so they need to agree on a film. Ahmet uses the mouse and asks his friend.
Ahmet: (smiling warmly) Well, what do you think of the films, Özlem?
Özlem: (sighing) None of them is my cup of tea. To be honest, I like biographical films, and 
there’s nothing like that on the list.
Marcelo sips from his cup of coffee and puts his cup on the table.
Marcelo: (ironically) You? Biographical films? Interesting… Never thought you were into boring 
stuff like that.
Özlem: (warmly smiling) Marcelo, I’ll send you my favourite films, and please, watch them; 
you’ll agree with me.
Paloma opens her eyes excitedly, with the pride of watching this kind of film.
Paloma: (excitedly) Definitely, I also enjoy biographical films. They explore the lives of famous 
people. These people are an inspiration to the whole world.
Özlem: (appreciatively) I’m glad to hear that, Paloma. Especially when the actor or actress 
delivers an incredible performance, you feel really inspired.
Marcelo: (confidently) OK. It’s not my cup of tea anyway. I’m into mystery and thriller films.
Ahmet: (curiously) Well, I love mystery films, too. Any film suggestions?
Marcelo: Yes, of course. It’s a mystery and a bit of a thriller about people going back and forth 
in a time loop.
Ahmet takes his pen and a piece of paper to note the name of the film to watch later.
Marcelo: (continues) It’s on the tip of my tongue; er, anyway, I’ll text you.   
Ahmet: I’m really curious; I will watch it soon, don’t forget to text me.
Marcelo: I believe you’ll love it. This film also has a wise plot, beautiful scenes, and it keeps you 
watching until the very end. It is perfect for mystery lovers like me.
Özlem feels a bit bored and plays with her hair, twirling her hair.
Özlem: What about you, Paloma? What’s your favourite film genre?
Paloma: (enthusiastically) I love romantic films; they usually tell true stories.
Ahmet: (warmly smiling) Yes, they also have a classic plot.
Paloma: Actually, they’re not always classic plots. You can really see the strong efforts of the 
full film cast and crew in these films.
Marcelo: (sarcastically) Well, the most attractive actors and actresses star in these films. They 
don’t usually represent reality at all. Am I wrong?
Everybody looks into Marcelo’s eyes astonishingly and confusedly, showing how he judges these films.
Özlem: How about you, Ahmet? What is your favourite genre?
