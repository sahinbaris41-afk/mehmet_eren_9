WB Audio Script 8.1
Listen to the audio and circle the poster of 
the genre that is not mentioned.
Johan: Do you like films, Minel?
Minel: Of course! I love watching films, 
especially the action, comedy, 
thriller, and adventure ones. What 
about you?
Johan: Same here! But lately, I’ve been 
really into science fiction. Sci-fi films 
are amazing because they make 
us imagine life in the future with 
space adventures, time travel, alien 
invasions, and robots.
Minel: Yes, sci-fi films always have such 
cool ideas. Have you seen that new 
one, Galactic Operation?
Johan: Yes! It’s about astronauts who 
discover a new planet. At first, 
they think it’s empty, but then they 
realise there are robotic creatures in 
it. They have to survive and explore 
the planet’s secrets. The special 
effects are incredible!
Minel: That sounds so cool! Do you think 
films will change a lot in the future?
Johan: Definitely! I think in the future, films 
will be more interactive. Maybe we’ll 
be able to get inside the stories with 
virtual reality glasses.
Minel: I agree! Maybe artificial intelligence 
will create new films without human 
actors. That would be so weird but 
also really interesting.
Johan: For sure! I also think we’ll explore 
space more in real life, so sci-fi films 
will become even more realistic.
Minel: I hope so! Well, what should we 
watch now?
Johan: How about a superhero film or 
maybe a space adventure?
Minel: Perfect! Let’s grab some popcorn 
and start watching!
