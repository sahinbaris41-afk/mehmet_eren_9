Ask students to play the game in two 
groups of four. Have them read the rules 
before starting the game. If needed, explain 
each step of the game in detail. While 
students are playing the game, monitor 
them and take notes of any grammar and 
target vocabulary mistakes they make. Then 
correct students’ mistakes using scaffolding 
questions, which means offering the class 
true and false options for a mistake and 
asking them to figure out their mistakes 
and correct them.
