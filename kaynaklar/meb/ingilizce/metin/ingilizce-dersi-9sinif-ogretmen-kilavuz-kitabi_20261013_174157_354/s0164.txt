EXT. COTTAGE – GARDEN – LATE AFTERNOON (DAY 5)
A warm sunset casts a glow over the garden. There is an elegantly set table with small decorations. 
Sharon, Quincy, Oliver, William, and Lucy sit around the table and wait for their guests.
Sharon: (noticing movement near the gate) Look, they’re here!
Sharon and Quincy stand up and walk towards the gate. Kate, Alan, and Burak arrive. They are 
smiling and carrying small gifts.
Quincy: Welcome! So glad you could come.
Sharon: Please come in. Make yourselves at home.
They open the gate, and the guests enter the garden. Sharon shows them to the table. As they 
approach the table, Oliver, William, and Lucy stand up to greet them.
