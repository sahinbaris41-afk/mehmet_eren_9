i   Ask students to work in pairs. Tell them that 
they are going to ask and answer questions 
as in the example. Remind them that they 
can write their questions and answers in 
their notebooks before taking turns doing 
the activity.
Suggested Answers
Does she wake up early? Yes, she does.
What time does she wake up? She wakes 
up at 7.30.
Does she walk to school? Yes, she does.
How often does she walk to school? She 
rarely walks to school. 
Does she watch TV series? No, she doesn’t.
What does she do at weekends? She goes 
swimming.

What time does he get out of bed? He gets 
out of bed at 7 o’clock.

he does.
What does he do at school? He participates 
in school activities.
Does he go shopping? No, he doesn’t.

after school.
