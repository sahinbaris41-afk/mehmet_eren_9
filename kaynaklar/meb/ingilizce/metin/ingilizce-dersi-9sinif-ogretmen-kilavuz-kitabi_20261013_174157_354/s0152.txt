CLASSROOM LIFE2 DIGITAL STORY SCRIPTS
Digital Story 2
The view of a street and a house. As the sun rises, the neighbourhood is calm. A dog is barking from 
afar.
INT. JACOB’S BEDROOME – EARLY MORNING
A smartphone screen appears as an alarm rings. Jacob, half-awake, reaches over to turn off the 
alarm. He sighs as he sits up slowly.
Jacob: (soft, thoughtful) Every day starts the same… I wake up bright and early and check the 
time. It’s seven a.m. in the morning.
Jacob rises as he stretches and blinks in the morning light. He begins making his bed by pulling the 
covers up neatly.
Jacob: It’s my favourite day of the week, Friday! First, I make my bed. I do it neatly because I 
like to keep my room tidy.
INT. BATHROOME – EARLY MORNING
Jacob splashes water on his face to wake up fully. He cleans his face with care, brushes his teeth, and 
combs his hair.
Jacob: Then, I go to the bathroom. I clean my face, brush my teeth, and comb my hair. (smiling) 
I look refreshed.
INT. BEDROOME – MOMENTS LATER
Jacob pulls his jacket from his wardrobe and gets dressed.
Jacob: I get dressed quickly.
His phone battery is low, so he plugs it in to charge quickly.
Jacob: I charge my phone every morning. I hope it’ll last all day.
INT. KITCHENE – EARLY MORNING
Jacob has breakfast at the counter. His mum shouts from another room.
Mum: Don’t forget to charge your phone, Jacob!
Jacob: (rolling his eyes) I already did, Mum!
Jacob takes his backpack, heads for the door, and steps outside.
Jacob: I usually leave home around the same time. I hope I can take the bus to school, or I’ll 
walk. I rarely walk to school.
EXT. STREET – MORNING
Jacob walks down the street and checks his watch. It shows 7.50 a.m. He arrives at the bus stop and 
boards quickly.
INT. SCHOOL BUSE – MORNING
Jacob finds a seat near the front. He puts in his headphones and stares out the window.
Jacob: Every Monday, every Tuesday… I have the same routine every day.
