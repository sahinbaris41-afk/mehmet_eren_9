Gamification
Ask students to play the game in groups 
of four. Each group member makes a 
sentence with the given words or phrases, 
using The Present Continuous Tense. The 
group with the most points when time is 
up wins the game. While they are playing 
the game, monitor them and take notes 
about their mistakes with the grammar 
structures, then explain the rules one more 
time if needed.
