WB Audio Script 4.1
Listen to the audio and complete the 
missing information in Lara’s project.
Lara: Mum, I need to write about jobs for 
my project. Can you help me?
Mum: Of course! What do you need to do, 
Lara?
Lara: I have to describe at least five family 
members’ jobs and their workplaces.
Mum: That sounds interesting. Okay, then 
let’s start with your uncle, Larry. He’s 
an architect. He designs buildings at 
an architecture firm.
Lara: Oh, I remember! He showed me 
some of his designs last summer. 
What about Aunt Lisa?
Mum: Aunt Lisa is a pharmacist. She works 
at a pharmacy and gives customers 
the correct medicine on their 
prescriptions.
Lara: That’s great! And what about your 
cousin, Jeremy?
Mum: Jeremy is a very successful lawyer. 
He has his own law firm, and he 
defends people in court.
Lara: I didn’t know that! What about 
Cousin Jeremy’s sister?
Mum: Oh, you mean Cousin Emily. She’s 
a journalist. She writes articles and 
reports the news for a big news 
agency. She always travels for work. 
That’s why we can’t see each other 
for a while.
Lara: That’s so sad! Hope you see her 
soon. What about Uncle Danny?
Mum: As far as I know, he’s a security 
guard at a bank. He checks IDs and 
protects people.
Lara: That’s a very important job. Thanks, 
Mum! This will help me a lot with 
my project. I think I want to be an 
architect like Uncle Larry.
Mum: That’s great! My pleasure, dear. You 
made me miss my relatives. I’ll call 
them at the weekend.
