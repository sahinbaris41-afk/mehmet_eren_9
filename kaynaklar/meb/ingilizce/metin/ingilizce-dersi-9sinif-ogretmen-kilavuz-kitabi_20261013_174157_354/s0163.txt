INT. COTTAGE – BEDROOM – CONTINUOUS
A bright bedroom with two single beds and two desks.
William: Look, there’s a view of the garden.
Oliver: This room is lovely and there are two single beds. (turning around) Mum, Dad, 
can this be our bedroom?
Sharon: (chuckles) We’ll see.
EXT. COTTAGE – AFTERNOON
The family and the Estate Agent stand by the “FOR SALE” sign.
Estate Agent: (smiling) So, what do you think? Is this the right house for you?
Sharon: We absolutely love it!
Quincy: (nods) Yes, we’ve decided. We want to buy it.
Estate Agent: That’s wonderful news!
She takes a “SOLD” sticker from her folder and places it over the “FOR SALE” sign.
Estate Agent: Let’s meet at my office tomorrow to sign the paperwork. What time suits you?
Quincy: How about 10 a.m.?
Estate Agent: Perfect. I’ll see you then. Congratulations again!
They wave goodbye, and the Estate Agent walks away.
INT. COTTAGE – DINING ROOM – AFTERNOON (DAY 4)
The family is moving in. Boxes are scattered around. Sharon is in the dining room, and she is preparing 
the table for lunch.
Sharon: (calling out) I’m preparing the table for lunch and need a hand. What are you all 
doing now?
Oliver and 
William: (shouting back together) We’re tidying up our room!
Lucy: (shouting back) I’m ironing the curtains!
Quincy: (washing dishes) I’m washing the dishes but I’ll be done in a minute. I can help 
you, Sharon!
Sharon: All right, thanks!
She remembers something and pauses.
Sharon: (to herself) Oh, I need to call Kate.
She picks up her phone from the table and dials. After two rings, Kate answers.
Sharon: Hi, Kate. How are you?
Kate: (on the phone) I’m well, thanks. How about you?
Sharon: (excited) We’ve finally got a new house and are moving in!
Kate: That’s fantastic! What kind of house is it?
