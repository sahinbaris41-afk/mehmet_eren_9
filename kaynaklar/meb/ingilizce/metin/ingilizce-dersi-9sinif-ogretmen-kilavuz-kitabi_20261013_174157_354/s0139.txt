events. There are so many different 
dishes from all over the world.

have?
Lena: Well, last year, they had Turkish 
pizza, tacos, and lots of seafood.

can also try sweet desserts like 
chocolate cake or coconut biscuits.
Eren: Amazing! I love spicy food! Are you

Lena: I think so. I usually eat burgers or 
pasta at festivals, but this time I

taste things, and take photos.
Eren: Great. I’ll see you there then!
