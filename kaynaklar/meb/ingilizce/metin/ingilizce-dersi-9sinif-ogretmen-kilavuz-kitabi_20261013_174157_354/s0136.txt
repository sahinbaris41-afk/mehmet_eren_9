 Renting a flat
Estate agent: Hello, this is Chance Realty. 
How can I help you?
Client: Hi, I’m interested in renting 
the two-bedroom flat in the 
city centre. Can I have some 
more details about it?
Estate agent: Of course! It’s on the second 
floor of a modern building. 
Client: That sounds nice. Does it 
have a balcony?
Estate agent: Yes, it has a small balcony 
with a great view of the park. 
You can also put a small 
table and chairs there.
Client: That sounds lovely! What 
about the living room? Is it 
furnished?
Estate agent: Yes, the living room has a 
sofa, an armchair, and a 
coffee table. There’s also a 
TV stand, but you should 
bring your own TV.
Client: That’s fine. And the kitchen? 
Does it have appliances?
Estate agent: Yes, there’s a fridge, a 
cooker, and a dishwasher. 
The cupboards are large 
and new, so there is a lot 
of storage for kitchen items.
Client: That’s good. Is there a dining 
room, or do I need to eat in 
the kitchen?
Estate agent: There’s no separate dining 
room, but the kitchen has 
space for a small dining 
table with four chairs.
Client: I see. What about the 
bedrooms?
Estate agent: Each bedroom has a bed, 
a wardrobe, and a bedside 
table. The master bedroom 
also has a chest of drawers.
Client: That’s great. And the 
bathroom?
Estate agent: It has a shower, a basin, and 
a cupboard for bathroom 
items.
Client: That sounds perfect. What 
about the heating? 
Estate agent: There’s central heating in the 
flat, so it stays warm during 
the winter. 
Client: Oh, that’s nice. Is there a 
garage?
Estate agent: There’s a garage, but spaces 
are limited. Oh, there’s also 
a parking area behind the 
building where you may find 
a space.
Client: Okay, I understand. When 
can I visit the flat?
Estate agent: I can show you the flat 
tomorrow at 3 p.m. Is that 
okay with you?
Client: Yes, that time works for me. 
Thank you!
Estate agent: You’re welcome! See you 
tomorrow.
