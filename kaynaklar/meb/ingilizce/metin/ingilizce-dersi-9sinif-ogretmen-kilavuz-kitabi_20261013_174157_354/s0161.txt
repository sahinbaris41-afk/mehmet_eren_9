DIGITAL STORY SCRIPTS
Digital Story 5
An estate agency office with large windows letting in morning light. An estate agent sits behind a 
neatly arranged desk with papers and brochures. She sits face to face with Sharon and Quincy.
Estate Agent: (smiling) So, you’re looking for a new home for your family? Where do you live at 
the moment?
Sharon: (nods slightly) We live on the top floor of a block of flats near the station on 
Queen’s Road.
Quincy: We’re hoping to find something with plenty of room for the kids. We have two 
twin boys and a girl.
Estate Agent: What’s your budget?
Quincy: It’s around £275,000.
Estate Agent: (pulls out a folder and starts flipping through pages) A flat is definitely not an 
option for you, so I’ll skip these. Let’s see… How about a cottage just outside the 
town? It has a lovely garden, a garage, and a terrace.
Sharon: Great! But is there a utility room?
Estate Agent: Yes, there is, and the cottage has three bedrooms, a sitting room, two 
bathrooms, and two toilets.
Sharon: What do you think, Quincy?
Quincy: Well, I do love the idea of a garden.
Estate Agent: There’s also a detached house in the countryside. It has a spacious kitchen with 
a big table, wooden cupboards, a fridge, and an oven. There’s also a dining 
room for formal meals.
Sharon: A detached house sounds interesting but it might be too far from the city 
centre.
Quincy: I agree. I think the cottage is more suitable for us.
Estate Agent: All right then. Let’s arrange a viewing of the cottage. How about this afternoon 
at 2 o’clock?
Sharon: That works for us. We’ll see you then.
The couple gets up, shakes hands with the estate agent, and heads towards the door.
EXT. COTTAGE – AFTERNOON
There is a cottage with a garden and a white fence. A gate leads to the front entrance. The Estate 
Agent waits by the “FOR SALE” sign near the gate with a folder in her hand. The family car pulls up.
Sharon, Quincy, and their children, Oliver, William, and Lucy, step out of the car.
Estate Agent: (smiling and gesturing towards the house) Welcome to your potential new 
home.
Quincy: (admiring the house) What a nice house! It’s even better than I imagined.
